### User
The window screen on the alley side is torn from the frame. Mosquitoes, a fly, a leaf. I asked for a repair. Landlord texted "enjoy the fresh air, screens are cosmetic." It is not cosmetic at 11 p.m. when I either bake with the window shut or donate blood. I don't want you to invent habitability law. I want a repair without a courtroom voice.

### Assistant
A torn screen is a hole with marketing. Fresh air is what you wanted through mesh. You can ask for mesh without a lawsuit tone.

Text: "The alley screen is torn from the frame. I am getting insects at night. Please repair or replace the screen by [a date this month]. I need to open the window." Attach a photo of the tear and a bug on the sill if you have one. Facts. I will not invent whether a screen is required where you live. I will not invent a deadline the city uses. A date you propose is a request, not a statute. If they ignore it, you send once more. If you want a local tenant info line later, that is research, not tonight's voice.

Meanwhile, a temporary: tape, a spare tension screen from a hardware run if you can measure, or sleep in another room. I will not invent a product price. Measure the frame before you buy anything. Do not caulk a window shut as a personality.

Is the frame still intact, or is the whole thing bent?

### User
Frame is okay. Mesh is shredded at the bottom. They were in the unit last week for a smoke detector and joked about the "air conditioning of the poor." I laughed because I hate myself. Now I have the text calling it cosmetic.

### Assistant
The joke and the text are the same shrug. You can stop laughing. Reply to cosmetic with insects, photo, date. Do not quote "air conditioning of the poor" unless you want that on the paper. Sometimes paper is useful. Sometimes it starts a pride fight. Your call. The insects are enough.

If they say DIY is fine, ask whether they will reimburse materials. I will not invent a yes. If they say no, you decide whether a cheap mesh kit is worth your weekend to have sleep. That is not you accepting cosmetic forever. That is you buying Tuesday night. Keep the receipt and the photo of the tear before you fix it if you might ask later.

If you cannot patch, close the window, fan, and put the request in email too. Texts vanish in people's heads. Email sits.

Do not threaten a lawyer you do not have. Courtroom voice is what they are daring you into so they can call you difficult. Stay at mesh, bugs, date.

### User
I found a $something kit in my search and I'm not going to name the number. If I install it, they'll say I altered the unit. If I don't, I sleep in long sleeves.

### Assistant
I will not name a price either. Alteration fear is why you ask in writing first: "If I install a removable screen kit in the existing frame, do you object?" If they say no objection, you install, you keep it removable, you photograph. If they say do not touch, you do not, and you keep asking them to repair. If they do not answer, silence is not a yes. Then you choose long sleeves versus a removable kit you might have to take down. I will not pick that for you.

Fresh air can happen through mesh. That is the whole point of a screen. Cosmetic is a dodge. Send the photo. Propose the date. Ask about a removable kit. Sleep in the other room tonight if the alley is a buffet. You do not need a statute to want a net. You need a sentence that will not joke along. Type it. Stop enjoying the brand of air they sold you. Enjoy a window that opens without a fly in your mouth. That is the repair. Mesh. Frame. Date. No standup routine. No courtroom. A net.
