### User
Mom wants me to "talk to your brother" again. He skipped Dad's birthday. Dad is icing him. Mom is using me as the hallway. I have done this every holiday since I was sixteen. I am twenty-nine. I don't want a script that fixes them. I want a first-time sentence that I am not the peacemaker, and I want to say it without a diagnosis of the family. Sunday lunch is in two days.

### Assistant
You can refuse the hallway job without explaining their marriage or your brother's calendar. The first-time sentence is: "I'm not going to be the go-between. If you want to talk to him, you call him." Then you stop. Stopping is the part you have never done.

Do not offer to "just pass a message." Passing is the job. Do not offer to host them in separate rooms. Hosting is the job with extra chairs. I will not diagnose anyone. I will not invent who is right about the birthday. I was not there. You do not need a verdict to drop the referee whistle.

If she cries, you can say, "I know you want it smooth. I'm still not carrying it." Care and the job are different. You can love her and not phone your brother with a prepared paragraph.

Sunday: you can go to lunch as a guest. Guest means you eat, you talk about the food, you do not run shuttle diplomacy between the salad and the cake.

Has she already asked you to place a call this week, or is it still a "you'll handle it" assumption?

### User
She asked me to call him tonight and "make him come on Sunday." She said if I don't, lunch will be ruined and it will be on me. I haven't called. I can feel the old voice forming.

### Assistant
Do not make him come. You cannot make a grown person attend a lunch. The old voice is a booking agent. Let it die in the draft.

Text Mom tonight, not a performance call: "I'm not calling him to make him come. I'm not the peacemaker. I will be at lunch. You can invite him yourself." If you prefer voice, say the same three sentences and hang up before the negotiation. Negotiation is how sixteen-year-old you got the job.

If she says it will be on you, you can say, "It won't. I'm not running this." Then you let Sunday be awkward if it is awkward. Awkward lunch is a real outcome. It is still not your personal failure. Ruined is a story people tell to keep a courier.

Do not secretly text your brother a warning either, unless you want a side channel. Side channels are the job in a hoodie. If he asks you what he should do, you can say, "That's between you and them. I'm not advising." Same door, other relative.

### User
Dad will ask me in the kitchen why I didn't fix it. He does the quiet version. I freeze when he does that.

### Assistant
Kitchen, quiet, freeze: have one line in your pocket before you park. "I'm not fixing it. I came to eat." Then you pick up a dish or go back to the table. Hands busy. If he waits in silence, you can let silence sit. You do not have to fill it with a plan. Filling is the freeze breaking the wrong way.

If you skip lunch entirely, that is another option, but you already said you will be there. Be there as a diner. Leave when the peacemaking starts if you have a ride. "I need to go" is allowed even if the pie is still whole.

First time will feel rude in your body. Rude is often just new. You are not issuing a family diagnosis. You are declining a role. Call no one tonight. Text Mom the three sentences. Sunday, eat, one kitchen line, no shuttle. The birthday already happened. You cannot restage it as a twenty-nine-year-old intern. Sit down. Pass the salt, not the peace.
