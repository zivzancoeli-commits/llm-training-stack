### User
Jordan keeps putting sticky notes on my yogurt. Today's said "this is a cry for help, buy real food." Yesterday's was a drawing of my name as a sad banana. I don't think they're stealing. I think they think it's a bit. I hate it. I don't want to be the roommate who can't take a joke, and I also don't want notes on my breakfast. How do I shut this down without a house meeting?

### Assistant
It is your food and your shelf. A joke that needs your yogurt as a billboard is still using your stuff. You can shut it down in the kitchen, not in a summit.

One line on the actual next note, or a text if that is how you two talk: "Please stop putting stickies on my food. I don't like it." That is not a bit. That is the request. Do not add "it's fine if it's funny" because that invites a quality contest. You do not want better jokes. You want blank lids.

I will not invent house rules you signed. If you have a chore chart, this is not on it. If you don't, you still get to say no to decorations on your groceries.

Take the notes off and throw them away. Do not archive them on the fridge as a comedy wall. Archiving trains the bit.

Do they only hit your stuff, or is the whole fridge a sticky gallery?

### User
Only mine. They said my food "looks lonely." We split groceries sometimes but yogurt is mine. I laughed the first time because I was tired. Now it's a streak and they asked if I liked today's caption.

### Assistant
The laugh was a first-time accident. The streak is them checking for a yes. Today's "did you like the caption" is a prompt. Answer the prompt with the real no, not a rating.

Text or in person, tonight: "I laughed once because I was tired. I don't want notes on my food. Please stop. Yogurt is mine. I'm not doing the bit." If you want one pressure valve, you can add, "If you want to joke, joke at me, not on the foil." Only add that if you actually will tolerate jokes aimed at your face. If you will not, skip the valve. Empty lid is the goal.

If they say you cannot take a joke, you do not defend your sense of humor. "I can take a joke. I don't want stickies on my food." Repeat once. Then you are done. A house meeting is what people call when a sticky note needed a speech. It needed a sentence.

If a new note appears tomorrow, you remove it, take a photo if you want a record, and send the same sentence. You do not write a rebuttal on a sticky. Do not start a note war. Wars are still notes on yogurt.

### User
They'll sulk. They sulk when I ask them to wash the cutting board too. Also I don't want them to start eating it out of spite.

### Assistant
Sulk is their weather. It is not a veto. Washing the cutting board and leaving your yogurt alone are both ordinary. You can let them be quiet in the living room. You do not have to host a repair conversation about a sticky note.

Spite-eating: if they eat your yogurt, that is a different problem than jokes. Name it if it happens: "That was mine. Replace it." I will not invent a price. Replace means the same item, not a speech about theft law. If it does not happen, do not pre-accuse. Pre-accusing is how a small boundary becomes a trial.

Put your name on the lid if it is not there. Name is dull and useful. Dull is your friend.

Send the stop text tonight. Peel tonight's banana drawing. Do not rate the caption. If they sulk, you still eat breakfast off a clean cup. That is the whole shutdown. No house meeting. No second bit. Blank yogurt.
