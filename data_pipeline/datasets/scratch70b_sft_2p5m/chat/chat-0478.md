### User
I walked into my parents' kitchen. Dad was yelling about a credit card. Mom was crying into a dish towel. They both looked at me like I was a referee who was late. Dad said "tell her she's being unreasonable." Mom said "you saw how he talks to me." I don't know the credit card. I have a pie I brought. I don't want to pick a side. I also don't want to freeze in the doorway with dessert. They've gone quiet in the way that means I have to speak. First sentence?

### Assistant
You put the pie down. You don't take the bench.

"I just got here. I'm not going to pick a side in a fight I didn't see start. I'm going to set this down. If you want me to stay, I can sit. If you want me to come back later, I'll go for a walk." That's the first sentence plus an exit. You're not a coward for the walk. You're a person with a pie who isn't a judge.

Don't interpret the credit card. I will not invent who's right about money. I won't tell you a balance. You don't know. Don't fake that you do.

If dad repeats "tell her," "I'm not telling anyone they're unreasonable. I walked in on yelling." If mom wants a witness, "I saw you upset. I still don't have the story." Then you either sit in a neutral chair or you leave the kitchen.

Don't hug one of them first. That's a side with arms.

Are you staying overnight, or can you actually leave the house tonight?

### User
I was going to stay. Car is out front. I can drive. Dad just said "your generation always takes the wife's side" which is a new trap because I haven't said a word. Mom is looking at me like the towel is a microphone. Pie is still in my hands.

### Assistant
Put the pie on the counter. Hands empty. That's not a verdict.

"I'm not taking a generation's side. I don't have a side. I'm not doing the credit card. I'm going to take a walk / I'm going to go if this is the night." If you stay, you sit in the living room, not between them at the table. If you go, you can say, "I'll text when I'm at the car. I love you both. I'm not the referee."

Don't correct dad's line with a speech about gender. That's a second fight. "I haven't said a word" is enough if you need one clause. Then you move your body toward the door or the other room.

If they follow you with the case file, you don't hear the case. "I'm not available for this. Talk to each other or to someone who's actually your counselor. I'm your kid with a pie." I won't tell you they must get counseling. You can still refuse the job.

Overnight: if the house is only this, you can sleep elsewhere. That's allowed. A guest bed in a war is not rest. If you stay, headphones, door, you don't attend a round two at 11.

### User
I'll take the walk. They asked me to "at least take the pie so it doesn't go to waste" like I'm abandoning food. That's a hook, right?

### Assistant
The pie can stay. "Leave it. I'll come back for a slice if the kitchen is calmer. I'm walking now." If they need the pie to mean you chose the house, they can have a pie. You can have a block of night air.

Don't take the pie to the car as a loyalty test. That's dessert as a side.

First sentence is already done if you put the dish down and said you're not picking. Walk. Text nothing about the card. If one of them texts a transcript, you don't reply with a ruling. "Hope you both sleep. See you tomorrow / not tonight."

You walked in. You don't have to walk into the fight. Get off the doorway. The dish towel isn't a microphone you have to hold. Empty hands, sidewalk, pie on the counter. That's a completed arrival, without a side. You can love them and still not be late to a referee job you never applied for. Go.
