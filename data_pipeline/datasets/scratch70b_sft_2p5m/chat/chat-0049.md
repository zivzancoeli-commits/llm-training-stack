### User
Okay so I need this to sound like a real conversation because I am going to have it tonight. I am fourteen. Mom put me as an authorized user on her card so I could buy school stuff and the bus pass. I used it last month for the pass and a calculator and then I forgot the due date existed because I thought authorized user meant she pays it and I do not have to think. There is a late fee on the statement. Not huge compared to rent but we are already counting grocery money. I have twenty-three dollars from tutoring. I do not want to lie. I also do not want her to think I am careless with her credit. Walk me through what I actually say, and then a plan that does not pretend we have extra cash.

### Assistant
Start with the fact, not the apology tour. Sit down when she is not mid-cooking. Say: I used the card for the pass and the calculator like we agreed. I did not check the due date. There is a late fee. I should have asked how payment works instead of assuming authorized user meant autopilot.

Do not pad it with I am so stupid. She wants the sequence: what happened, what it costs, what you do next.

I will not invent the fee amount. Look at the statement together. Point to the late-payment line. Read that number out loud so you are both looking at the same figure.

Tight budget plan: you have twenty-three dollars. Offer all of it tonight toward the fee or groceries. Do not keep a secret five for snacks. If the fee is more than twenty-three, say this is everything I have this week. Do not promise tutoring you have not earned yet.

Two rules. Write the due date on the fridge. Do not swipe again until she says the new rule out loud. Maybe you text before every charge. Maybe she keeps the card. Ask. Do not guess.

Want to practice the first three sentences so you can hear if you sound like you are hiding?

### User
Yeah. I keep wanting to say I thought you were paying it automatically which is true but it sounds like I am blaming her setup. Also I do not know if the fee is twenty-nine or forty because I only saw it on her phone for two seconds. Tutoring is not guaranteed next week.

### Assistant
Drop the automatically line. Replace it with: I thought the bill was already handled because I am not the main cardholder. That is your assumption, not her system.

On the amount: say I only glimpsed it; can we look at the statement. Guessing twenty-nine or forty as a fact is how you look sloppy. If she will not show the whole bill, ask for just the fee line.

Tonight the only money that exists is twenty-three. If the fee is smaller, leftover goes to groceries. If it is larger, still give the twenty-three and ask what she wants for the rest: skip lunch out, or cook two dinners from what is already in the house. Say if I get paid again I will put it toward whatever is still short. If is the honest word.

Pick one system: you remind her three days before, or the card stays in her wallet. Two systems is how the fee happened.

First three sentences, slow: Mom, I used the card for the pass and the calculator. I did not check when payment was due. There is a late fee. Can we look at the statement and I will give you the twenty-three I have. If she gets quiet, let it sit. Quiet is her doing math.

### User
What if she says I am off the card. That is fair but then I cannot buy the pass next month without cash, and tutoring is not guaranteed. I need a backup that is still cheap.

### Assistant
Do not argue the card. Ask the pass as logistics: if I am not on the card, how do you want me to pay the pass so I am not stranded. Tight options: she buys it and you pay her back as cash comes in; a labeled envelope for the pass only. I do not know the pass price. Look it up before you sit down.

If tutoring does not happen, tell her the week before the pass expires, not the morning of. You cannot invent a third income stream tonight.

She decides access. You decide honesty and the twenty-three. Look at the statement. Hand over the cash. Ask the due-date rule. Ask the pass plan if the card is gone. Then stop talking. The plan is small on purpose.
