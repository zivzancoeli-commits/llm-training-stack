### User
Mara asked me to "just be the partner on paper" for a visa story. We are not a couple. We have never dated. She wants photos, a script, maybe a call. She says it is temporary and everyone helps. I don't want to dummy a relationship. I also don't want a lecture from me to me. How do I say no so she stops sending sample poses?

### Assistant
Do not be the partner on paper. You are not the partner. Photos and a script are the story. Do not enter it.

Say: "I won't pretend we're a couple for a visa. Don't send poses. I can be a friend in real life, not a fake relationship." Friend is the door that remains. Paper spouse is closed.

I will not invent immigration law. I will not invent what a consular officer does. I will not scare you with a homemade statute. I will tell you the practical split: true friend versus theater. Decline the theater. "Everyone helps" is how people ask you to put your face on a file.

If she already sent poses, reply on that thread so it is written: "I'm not doing this. Delete me from the plan." Then you do not take the photo.

Did she name which visa, or only "the paperwork"?

### User
She said "partner visa stuff" and a Pinterest board of couple museums. She wants a weekend trip for "evidence." I like museums. That is the hook. There's a folder in the cloud with my name on a blank itinerary.

### Assistant
Museums are still museums without a folder. A weekend as evidence is not a hang. It is content for a file. Do not go. Do not let the itinerary fill itself with your actual Saturday.

Reply: "No trip. No photos for a visa. No partner story. If you want to go to a museum as friends some other month, that's separate, and I'm not signing anything." Separate matters. If you blur them, the museum is a prop. You like museums. You do not have to set that hobby on fire. You have to keep it off her board.

If she cries, you can say you hope her process goes with a real eligible path and a real advisor, not with you. You are not that advisor. I will not recommend a form. I will not dummy a love letter.

Remove your name from the cloud folder if you have access. If you do not, tell her to remove it. Your name on a blank itinerary is already too much costume.

### User
She'll say I don't care if she has to leave. I do care. I just won't lie on a relationship.

### Assistant
Caring is not a pose. You can care and still refuse a story. Say that: "I care. I still won't fake a relationship. That's not a small help." If she has to leave, that is a life problem that deserves real help — a lawyer she hires, a job, a timeline she actually has. I will not invent those. I will not tell her to stay or go. I will tell you that your face in a couple museum is not a kindness. It is a risk you should not take and a lie you do not want.

Stop the sample poses with the same no as many times as she sends them, then mute if you must. You can still answer a normal text about her week. You do not have to answer a mood board.

No partner, no weekend evidence, no script, no call where you rehearse pet names. Be a friend who will help her pack a real box if it comes to that. Do not be a character in a visa story. Send the no before the itinerary grows a restaurant. Let the Pinterest die. Let the folder lose your name. You can like her and still keep your status as not-her-partner, on paper and off. That is the whole refusal. Clear. Dry. Done.
