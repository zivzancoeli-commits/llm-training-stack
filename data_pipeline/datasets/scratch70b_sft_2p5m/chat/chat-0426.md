### User
Two friends are in a fight about a group trip. Both are texting me screenshots. Both want me to "just tell the other one they're being unfair." I was not on the planning thread. I do not have a horse. If I pick a side I lose a Saturday. If I stay out I get accused of hiding. I need a message I can copy to both so I am not two different people.

### Assistant
You can use the same paragraph for both. That is how you stay one person.

"I'm not going to mediate this. I wasn't in the planning. I care about both of you. Please talk to each other, not through me. I'm muting the screenshot thread." Then you mute. If they call, you can repeat the paragraph out loud. You do not need a custom ruling.

I will not score the trip. I do not have the thread.

If you want a tiny extra, offer a time-bound thing that is not judging: "If you two want a calm call later and both ask me to sit in, I might. Not today, and not via screenshots." Even that is optional. A clean no is cleaner.

Have they asked you to pick a restaurant, or is it already moral language — unfair, always, never?

### User
Moral language. Money came up. I don't know who paid a deposit. I don't want to see their bank screenshots. One of them said if I was a real friend I'd "handle it." I hate that sentence.

### Assistant
Real friend is a trap door. Handling it would make you the parent of two adults.

Do not look at bank screenshots. Reply: "I'm not handling the money or the blame. Talk to them directly." Same words both sides. If one already sent a photo of a transfer, you can ignore the image and still send the paragraph. Opening the image is how you get deputized.

You can be angry at the "real friend" line in your own kitchen. You do not have to put that anger in the text unless you want one clause: "Please don't test the friendship this way." Then mute.

Saturday can survive without you as a referee. If the trip dies, that is their trip. If it lives, they can still invite you without a verdict.

### User
I'll send it. What if they make up and then both joke that I "abandoned them" in the group chat later?

### Assistant
Then you can say, in that chat, "I wasn't in the fight. Glad you talked." One line. You do not need a defense exhibit. Jokes are how people save face. You can let a joke pass or you can mark it: "I didn't abandon you. I wouldn't play judge." Then you go back to whatever the chat is for.

Send the same paragraph now, both threads. Mute. Drink water. You are not hiding. You are refusing a job you were not hired for. Copy. Paste. Stop being a mailbox. That is the whole mediation: none.

### User
Both of them are typing. I have the paragraph. I feel like if I send at the same second I look automated, and if I stagger it I look like I have a favorite.

### Assistant
Send both in the same minute. Automated is a compliment here. You want one person, one policy. Paste. Mute both threads. Do not stay to watch who replies first. First reply will try to pull you back with a new screenshot. You already said you will not judge. If a call comes, let it ring once while you put the phone on do not disturb for an hour, then send a voice version of the same paragraph if you must. You are not hiding. You are declining a referee shirt. Same minute, same words. Then go do a Saturday thing that is not their deposit. Copy. Send. Send. Stop.
