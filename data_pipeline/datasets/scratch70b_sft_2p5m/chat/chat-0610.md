### User
The park fountain across the street has a pump that rattles after 10. I reported it three times. The city site says "received." Nobody comes. Tonight it sounds like a dishwasher with a bolt in it. My bedroom faces the basin. They will not service it. I don't want you to invent a municipal code. I want a next step that is not a fourth copy-paste into a black hole, and I want to sleep.

### Assistant
Three "received" tickets is a pile, not a repair. You can stop believing the portal is a person. Sleep is still the first job tonight.

Close the window, fan, plugs, back room if you have one. Then one new tactic, not a fourth identical form: a photo and a twenty-second video from your sidewalk, date visible, attached to a reply on the existing ticket if the site allows comments. "Still rattling after 10, third follow-up, bedroom facing basin, please close the fountain or send a crew." Same facts, proof. I will not invent a quiet-hour law. I will not invent a fine. A pump that sounds like a bolt can be a maintenance item even in a city that loves fountains.

If there is a parks number that a human answers in daylight, use daylight. Tonight you will get a recording. Do not stand at the fence yelling at water.

Have they ever actually turned it off at night, or does it run 24 hours by design?

### User
It used to shut off around 9. This summer it just runs. There's a locked cabinet at the back with a city sticker. I am not going to pry it. My neighbor wants to unplug "whatever we can reach." I need you to tell me not to do a crime if that's the advice.

### Assistant
Do not pry the cabinet. Do not unplug a city pump. You do not know what else is on that circuit, and you do not want to be the person in a camera still with a flashlight in a parks box. Tell your neighbor no. The next step is still paper and daylight, not a freelance electrician bit.

If the fountain used to die at 9, say that in the ticket. "Previously off at 9, now overnight, rattle since [month]." A change is easier for a desk to route than a vibe. If you have an old video from a quieter night, even better. If you do not, the new clip is enough.

Council district email, if you know it, can be a second inbox in the morning. One paragraph, ticket numbers, video link. I will not promise a staffer cares. I will promise a black-hole portal likes company.

Tonight: no cabinet, no unplug, plugs, maybe a white-noise app you already have. Accept a bad sleep. Tomorrow: comment on the old tickets, not a fourth new one if the system duplicates. Duplicates let them close three and ignore one.

### User
If I email a council office I'll look like a person who complains about parks. I like the park. I hate the bolt.

### Assistant
You can like the park and hate the bolt in the same sentence. "I use the park. The overnight pump rattle is new. Tickets [numbers] are stuck on received. Please have parks shut it off at night or fix the mount." That is a user, not a villain. If they still file you as a grouch, you still asked for a machine to stop chewing itself.

If nothing moves for weeks, you can look at whether a window insert or a thicker curtain helps your particular glass. I will not invent a product or a price. I will not tell you to move. Mitigation at home is allowed while a city sits on received.

Tonight you do not become maintenance. You go to bed on purpose. Morning you send the clip and the old shutoff time. Neighbor stays away from the sticker. Fountain can look pretty at noon. After 10 it can be a basin of still water. That used to be the deal. Ask for the deal back, with video, once, in a human inbox. Then sleep as best the bolt allows. You already did the portal. Now you do proof and a person.
