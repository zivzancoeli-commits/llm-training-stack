### User
Work happy hour. Someone ordered a round of shots for the table. There's a tray. My director is holding one toward me. Last year I took it, hated it, and still took the second. I don't want it. I don't want a speech about not drinking. I also don't want "I'm driving" when I'm not. This is the first time I want a clean no at a work table. The lime is already on the napkin. Ten seconds.

### Assistant
Hands stay down. "I'm good, thanks — I'll skip this round." If you have a glass of soda, lift that instead. You don't need a reason. You don't need driving. You don't need antibiotics. Last year's you used props. Tonight you can have a no.

If they say "just one," "Still skipping. Cheers though." You can toast with the soda without the shot. If the director looks briefly odd, they can. You don't have to manage their toast.

Don't explain recovery or religion or a diet unless you want that to be the office lore. "I don't do shots" is enough if they push. I will not diagnose you. I won't invent a health claim. You don't want the tray. That's the job.

Don't take it and leave it. That's a prop and a spill. Don't pour it in a plant. That's a different story.

Is this your director or a peer holding the glass, and is anyone filming?

### User
Director. A peer is filming "for the team channel" which is worse. The tray is in front of me. I said nothing yet. I can feel the old swallow.

### Assistant
Don't swallow. Look at the director, not the camera. "I'm going to skip the shot. Please don't put me in the video if you're filming the round." Then you angle slightly out of the frame. If they still film, you can say it again to the peer: "No video of me with the tray." You don't need a legal lecture. You can still not want the channel.

If the director says "don't make it weird," you already didn't. A no is shorter than a shot face. "Not weird. I'm good with this soda." Then you ask someone a work-adjacent question so the table has a track: the project, the food, the train. You're still at the event. You didn't leave. That's not a protest. That's a drink choice.

If they put the glass in your hand, you can set it down. That's allowed. You don't have to knock it over. Set it down. "For someone else."

The old swallow is a muscle. You don't owe it to a director. Hierarchy isn't a throat.

### User
I set it down. Someone said "okay, mom." A few people laughed. My face is hot. I still have to sit here for the appetizers. What do I do with the next two minutes so I don't take the next tray to make it stop?

### Assistant
Let "mom" sit. Don't joke back "I'm fun, I swear." That's how the second tray happens. Eat a fry. Talk to the person who didn't laugh. If you need a bathroom, take it. Two minutes. Water.

You don't have to win the table. You have to not pick up the next glass. If another round comes, same sentence, shorter: "Still no thanks." Then the fry.

First time is allowed to be hot in the face. You declined at a work table without a fake car. That's the whole shot. Stay for the appetizer if you want the event. You already did the hard ten seconds. Don't pay for "mom" with tequila. Sit. The tray can move on. You can still be on the team without being in the video. Cheers with the soda if you need a gesture. Then let the lime be someone else's.

If the peer posts the video anyway, you can ask them once to cut you out. You don't have to write HR from the bar. One ask. Then you eat. The director can have a shot. You can have a fry. Both can exist at the same table. That's a work event, not a test you failed by keeping your hands down.
