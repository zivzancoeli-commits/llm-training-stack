### User
I am at the rental counter. The reservation was for 11:00. It is 12:20. They have no car in the class I booked and "maybe 45 minutes." The wedding is two hours away, ceremony at 4:00. I still have to change clothes. I am the person bringing the rings because the couple asked a friend, not a child. If I am late I am not just a guest. I need a decision tree: wait, switch company, rideshare, or call the couple. I don't want invented prices.

### Assistant
Rings beat a preferred car class. Start the clock from 4:00 and work backward: two hours of travel, plus a buffer for traffic and clothes. If 45 minutes here is real, you are already tight. If it is a maybe, treat it as longer.

Ask the counter, once, for the fastest car they can actually hand you now, any class. If they have keys, take them. Matching the reservation is a later refund conversation. I will not invent what they owe you.

If they have no keys, do not stand in a hopeful line. Step aside and look at the next way to move: another rental desk in the same garage, a train if one exists, a ride from a person, a rideshare if that is available where you are. I will not quote fares.

Call the couple or the maid of honor as soon as you know you might miss the processional. Rings in traffic need a backup human at the venue.

Are the rings on you right now, and is there anyone already near the venue who could receive them?

### User
Rings are in my inside pocket. The best man is at the hotel, twenty minutes from the venue. I could get to him if I leave now without a car, but I'm in an airport-ish lot. Counter just said 30 more minutes. My nice shoes are in the suitcase I was going to put in the trunk.

### Assistant
Thirty more minutes is a story. Decide with the time you have, not the story.

If a ride can pick you up now, go. Text the best man: you may hand him the rings at the hotel or at a pin drop. Do not hide the delay to be polite. Polite is how ceremonies start without rings.

Shoes can change in a bathroom later. Rings cannot. If the suitcase is a delay, take a smaller bag or wear what you have and change at the hotel. Vanity is not the constraint. The clock is.

If you wait at the counter, set a hard leave time: if there are no keys at 12:40, you walk away from the reservation and you move. Tell the agent you need a yes or no at that minute, not another maybe.

I will not tell you which company to try. I will tell you not to open a complaint essay while the lot has no car. Essays are for Sunday.

### User
If I call the couple I will scare them on their day. If I don't and I'm late, I scare them worse. What's the smallest true update?

### Assistant
Smallest true update, to the best man first, not to the couple if he can absorb it: "Rental delayed. I have the rings. I'm moving. I'll meet you at [hotel/venue] by [time I can defend]. If I'm not there by [earlier buffer], I'll hand off to a ride." Let him decide whether the couple needs to know. Many couples prefer the best man to know and the aisle to stay calm.

If you will miss the start even with a miracle, the couple deserves a short truth: "I might be late. Rings are with me, heading to [name] as backup." Then you stop. No saga about classes of cars.

Leave the maybe-counter if the keys are not in your hand. Get the rings closer to the best man. Change shoes later. You are a courier with a time problem. Solve the courier part. The wedding can survive a less pretty car. It cannot survive a pocket still in a lot. Move.
