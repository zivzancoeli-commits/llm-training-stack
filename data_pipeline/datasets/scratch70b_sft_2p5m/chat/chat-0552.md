### User
A friend added me to Group A. Then Group A spawned Group B for "the real chat." Then someone made a thread for photos, a thread for planning, and a silent group that only exists so nobody misses a poll. I am in five chats about one birthday. My phone is a slot machine. They want me in a sixth because "you're the organized one." I am considering throwing the phone in a drawer. How do I get out without becoming the flaky one.

### Assistant
You can leave chats. Organized is not a life sentence.

Mute is the first tool if you still need the date and the address. Leave is the tool if the sixth group is just more of the same. You don't need a speech in each thread.

To the friend who called you organized: "I'm staying in the one chat that has the time and place. I'm going to leave the extras. Don't add me to more." Direct, to them, not a broadcast manifesto that becomes a seventh topic.

Flaky is not showing up. Leaving photo-threads is hygiene. You can still bring a present.

I will not invent etiquette law for group texts. People survive unsubscribes.

Which of the five actually has the birthday facts, or is the information scattered on purpose?

### User
Scattered on purpose. Time is in B. Address got dropped in the photo thread. Poll is in the silent one. I only know this because I went hunting. Friend says if I leave I'll "miss the vibe." I do not want the vibe. I want the door code.

### Assistant
Ask for the facts in one message, then mute or leave.

"Send me time, address, door code in a DM. I'm stepping out of the extra groups." Then you do it. If they refuse to DM because vibe is the point, you already know this party is a job. You can still go if you want, with the facts, or skip if the cost is your notification tray.

Missing the vibe is allowed. Vibe is infinite messages. A door code is finite.

When you leave, you don't have to post "taking a digital break." That's an invitation to debate wellness. Silent leave, then the DM. If someone pings "why did you leave," you repeat the one-chat policy. Once.

Organized people get used as infrastructure. You can decline the promotion.

### User
They already named the sixth group "ops" and added me. I haven't opened it.

### Assistant
Leave it unopened. Opened is how you get assigned. Leave the group, send the DM for time/address/code, mute the one you keep if it still pings junk.

If they add you back, leave again and say "please don't re-add me." Two re-adds is you ignoring. You don't need to block a friend over a birthday. You need the door code and a quieter phone.

Drawer is a last resort for tonight if you're already fried. Tomorrow you still send the DM so you aren't actually flaky. Flaky is silence plus absence. You're aiming for presence without a pyramid. That's a reasonable phone.

### User
I left ops unopened. I left the photo thread. I DMed for time and address. They sent a door code and a guilt sticker. Four chats remain muted. I still see the badge counts in my peripheral vision.

### Assistant
Turn off badges for those threads if the app lets you, or archive them. Peripheral badges are how pyramids win after you already left. Door code in the DM is the prize. You already have it.

Guilt sticker gets no essay. If they ask you to "just stay for polls," you already answered. Organized is not on-call. You can still arrive on time with a present and a quiet phone.

Sixth group named ops was a job posting. You declined the job. Four muted leftovers can starve without you. Birthday still happens. You are not flaky for refusing a slot machine. You're a guest with a door code. That's the correct amount of group text.
