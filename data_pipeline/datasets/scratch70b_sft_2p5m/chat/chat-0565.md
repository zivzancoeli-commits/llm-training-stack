### User
Church next door had an event. It's 10:50 p.m. Parking lot is still full of idling cars, slam-doors, a speaker someone hasn't turned off, kids running. I like that the building is used. I also have a bedroom that faces the lot. They used to wrap by 9. I don't know if this is a one-off. I don't want to be the neighbor who hates church. Tonight and tomorrow, without a crusade.

### Assistant
Tonight is earplugs and a closed window if you can stand it. You don't walk into a parking lot of idling cars to lecture a congregation. That's a scene with no winner.

If the speaker is the spike, that's the detail to remember. Idling plus a speaker plus kids is a cluster. You don't need to hate the institution to want a lot to empty.

I will not invent a noise ordinance hour. Cities differ. Tomorrow you can look up the church office number, not a member's cell from a Facebook guess. "Lot was loud until 11 after the event. Bedroom faces it. Can last-out be quieter / earlier next time?" That's a neighbor note, not a lawsuit.

One-off versus pattern: you don't know tonight. Write the time down. If it never repeats, you slept badly once. If it repeats, you have dates.

Do you have a relationship with anyone who goes there, or is it only a wall and a lot?

### User
No relationship. There's a paper bulletin in their foyer I could read in daylight. Building group chat already said "typical" which isn't true — it used to end earlier. If I post, I'll get quoted as anti.

### Assistant
Don't post in the group chat. "Typical" is a shrug. Your email to the office is the channel. Anti is a chat-story. Offices answer facilities questions more often than they answer vibe.

Daylight bulletin: hours, a contact, maybe a calendar of late events. Useful. You can walk in when the office is open like a person, not at 10:50 like a protester.

Tonight, if something sounds unsafe rather than loud — a fight, someone hurt — that's a different call. Kids running in a lot is usually leftover event energy, not your emergency. Stay in.

Speaker-off is a reasonable ask even if cars take time to leave. People can talk without a PA at 11.

### Assistant
You can respect a service and still ask a parking lot not to be a concert at 11. If they frame it as expression, you don't debate theology. You repeat: bedroom, time, speaker, idling. Facilities language. Folding in a theological debate is how the PA wins. You didn't show up to win a faith argument.

Tonight: window, plugs, no lot confrontation, no group-chat. Tomorrow: office contact, one factual note, bulletin if you want dates. Church can keep being used. Your wall can still want quiet after the event. That's coexistence, not a crusade.
