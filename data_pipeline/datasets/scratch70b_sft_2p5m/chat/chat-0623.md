### User
I fasted from last night because they said morning labs. It is 10:40. They just called my name to say the clinician is running two hours behind and I can "keep waiting" or reschedule. I have a headache. I drank water. I don't want you to diagnose me or guess the test. I want to know how to talk to the desk without crying or pretending I'm fine to be convenient.

### Assistant
You already paid in hunger. Convenience is their word. You can be unfine at a desk.

Say: "I fasted for this. What are my options besides a two-hour wait?" Then listen for: stay and be seen later, reschedule with a fasting slot, or whether some of it can happen today without the clinician if labs are the bottleneck. I will not guess which applies. I will not name a test. I will not invent their policy. You want a fork, not a smile.

If you stay, ask whether you can wait in the car or a less bright hallway, and whether they will call your cell so you are not theater in a chair. If you leave, ask how soon a fasting-morning slot exists, and whether you should eat now. Eating now is obvious if you are done. If they might still poke you in twenty minutes, ask before you unwrap a bar. That is logistics, not a diagnosis.

Headache after a fast is a reason to sit, drink the water they allow, and not drive if you feel wrong. I am not telling you what is wrong. If you feel unsafe, that is a different desk sentence: you need help now. I will not triage from a chat.

Did they offer a new time, or only keep-waiting versus unspecified reschedule?

### User
Only wait or reschedule. The reschedule person is a voicemail. I took the morning off work. Parking is already paid. There's a vending machine I have been staring at. Other people are eating granola in the waiting room and I want to fight a granola bar.

### Assistant
Do not fight the granola. Fight the fork. Go back to the desk: "I took the morning off. Parking is paid. I fasted. I need either a real check-in time today or a booked fasting slot on the card before I leave. Voicemail isn't a slot." Then wait at the desk while they type. Sitting back down is how you become furniture again.

If they can give you "you're next after this one," that is a time-shaped object. If they cannot, you may eat. I will not tell you that eating ruins a lab. I will tell you to ask, "If I eat now, can you still do any of this today, or do I have to fast again?" Get that answer before the vending button. Then you can hate granola in peace.

Work morning off is a cost. You can say it once. Do not apologize for having a job. Do not cry on purpose. If you cry anyway, you can still finish the sentence. Staff have seen it. You are not a scene. You are a delayed fast.

Parking paid is sunk. It should not keep you hostage for two unstructured hours unless you choose to stay. Choose, do not leak.

### User
If I leave I wasted the fast. If I stay I might faint in a chair out of spite and physiology. I don't want either story.

### Assistant
A used fast is annoying. It is not a moral debt you must collect in a chair. Spite-fainting is not a plan. If you feel faint, tell the desk now, sit on the floor if you need a lower place, ask for water. That is not theater. That is a body.

Pick with the next hour, not with last night's dinner. If they produce a real time inside a window you can survive, stay, water, no vending until they answer the eat question. If they only have fog, eat, book the next fasting morning in person if a human can do it, go to work if you still can, or go home. Wasted fast becomes breakfast. Breakfast is allowed.

I will not diagnose the headache. I will not guess the order. I will not invent a lab rule. You wanted a conversation at the desk. Have it standing, with the fast named, with a slot or a release. Then either wait with a time or leave with food. Granola can remain other people's problem. Yours is a clock and a stomach. Use both. You already did the hard part overnight. Do not donate two more hours to a voicemail. Get a noun: time or exit. Then sit or eat on purpose.
