### User
Every morning my uncle forwards a video into the family WhatsApp. Today it was something about "they don't want you to know" and a news clip with captions that don't match. I was eating toast. My sister hearted it. My dad wrote "interesting." I wrote nothing. If I argue, breakfast becomes a debate club. If I leave the chat, I miss the actual family logistics — rides, recipes, who is picking up grandma. I don't want to be the hall monitor. I also don't want this on my phone at 8 a.m. What are my options that aren't a manifesto?

### Assistant
You have more options than "debate" or "exile."

Mute the chat. You still get the logistics if you check at lunch. Morning toast does not have to include a video. Muting is not leaving. It is a volume knob.

If you want a lighter filter, you can ask, in the chat, for two threads: one "family stuff," one "articles." Some families will do that. Many will not. Asking once is cheap. If they ignore it, you mute.

You do not have to rebut every forward. Rebutting trains the uncle to perform. A private text to your dad — "please don't 'interesting' this at breakfast; it spreads" — may do more than a public takedown.

I will not fact-check a video I cannot see, and I will not invent a law about forwards. I can say you are allowed to not engage.

Is the chat actually used for rides and grandma, or has it already become a broadcast channel with logistics as a side dish?

### User
Both. Grandma's appointments are in there. So are the videos. Uncle sends three on a good day. Sister hearts because she doesn't like conflict. Dad thinks he's being neutral.

### Assistant
Neutral is a heart's cousin. "Interesting" is a distribution method.

Mute, then set a daily reminder to scan for grandma. That protects toast and still gets you the Tuesday pickup. If an appointment is urgent, someone will call. They have been calling for decades.

If you stay unmuted, a once-a-week line is plenty: "Please keep medical and ride info in this chat. I'm skipping the videos." Then skip them. Do not click to be fair. Clicks are the product.

Do not write a point-by-point at 8:05. You will be late, and he will have another video at 8:20.

Sister can keep hearting. You can talk to her separately if you want an ally. You cannot make her stop. Dad can hear, once, that "interesting" reads as agreement to you. Then drop it. You are not converting the family before work.

### User
Uncle just asked in the chat "you all saw this right??" and my name is at the top because I was last online. It feels aimed.

### Assistant
It may be aimed. You can still not play.

If you must type, type a logistics thing you actually need, or type nothing. "Need grandma's Thursday time" is on-topic. "Saw it" is a hook. Do not take the hook.

If the red unread badge is the real torture, mute plus hide the chat on the home screen. Check at a set hour.

Leaving remains an option if the logistics can move to a smaller group with your sister and dad. Propose that once: "Can we do a small chat for grandma rides?" If they say yes, you have a quieter room. If they say no, you have mute.

Toast, then work. The video will still exist at noon. You are not the hall monitor. You are a person with a morning. Guard the morning. Let "you all saw this" hang in the air without your name on a reply. That is allowed, even if he pinged the top of the list. Mute before the next clip if you have not already. The badge can wait until you look for grandma's Thursday time on purpose.
