### User
The gray sedan in the lot has been yelping in ten-minute loops since 1:10. It's 2:40. Lights flash. No one comes out. I think it belongs to 3C because I've seen them unlock it. I don't want to pound on a door at 2:40. I also cannot keep doing the adrenaline nap. How do I handle a cycling alarm without inventing a towing law?

### Assistant
A cycling alarm is a machine stuck, not a personality. Treat it like a leak: find a human who can turn a key, then go back to bed.

I will not invent whether you may touch the car. I will not invent a towing rule. I will not invent a quiet-hour fine. Do not bang on the hood. Do not jiggle a handle. You can note the color, a plate if you can see it from your window, and the unit you think owns it.

If you are fairly sure 3C, a knock at 2:40 is allowed to be short: "Your car alarm has been cycling since 1:10. Please turn it off." Then you step back from the door. If they do not answer, a note and a morning knock. If the building has a night number or a super, that is the better 2:40 call: lot, gray sedan, looping, 3C maybe. Super has a clipboard. You have a ceiling.

Earplugs, fan, back bedroom if you have one. You will not get a pretty night. You can get a less electric one.

Do you have a super number that actually picks up, or only a portal?

### User
Portal. Super once answered at 6, never at 2. I don't want to mis-identify 3C and wake the wrong person. The lot camera is dummy, I think. My roommate wants to throw water on the windshield. Please tell them that's stupid if it is.

### Assistant
Throwing water is stupid. It can hit electrical parts, start a different fight, and still leave the alarm on. Tell your roommate no. You are not in a sitcom.

Mis-identifying 3C is a real risk. If you are not sure, do not knock a random door. Portal the super anyway with time and description so the morning has a ticket. If there is a building chat, a single factual line beats a pile-on: "Gray sedan in the back lot, alarm looping since 1:10, owner please." Then mute the chat. Chats at 2:40 become trials.

If you can see the car from a window and a person walks up with a fob, you can call down, not out onto the lot in socks. If nobody comes, you live with plugs and the portal.

I will not tell you to call a non-emergency police line. I do not know your city or whether they come for alarms. If you already know they do not, do not add a wait in the stairwell. If you already know they do, that is your local fact, not mine.

Roommate: water is a no. Recorded sound of the loop is optional for the portal, from your window, not from their bumper.

### User
3C's lights just flicked. Then off. Alarm still going. Roommate is putting shoes on to "have a talk."

### Assistant
Lights flick then off might be a TV. It might be a person who heard and rolled over. If your roommate goes, they go to 3C's door with the one sentence, not to the sedan to have a talk with glass. You can go with them so it does not become a speech, or you can make them stay. Two people at 2:40 can feel like a raid. One short knock is enough. If nobody answers, shoes come off. The talk waits for daylight when 3C is a face with coffee, not a myth with a fob.

Portal now so you are not relying on adrenaline memory. Then plugs. Then accept a bad night. Morning: knock if needed, super, not a neighborhood tribunal.

Stop the water plot. Stop the bumper talk. Key is the only off switch you do not have. Get as close as you can to a human with a key, then lie down. The sedan can yelp. You can refuse to yelp back. That is the whole night plan. Cycle, note, knock-or-don't, bed.
