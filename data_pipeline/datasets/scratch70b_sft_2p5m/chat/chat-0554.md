### User
A dresser has been in our hallway for eleven days. Neighbor in 4C said "just until the weekend" on day one. Weekend went. The dresser is against the railing. I turn sideways with groceries. I also keep thinking about whether this is a fire problem and then I stop because I don't want to sound like a hall monitor. I have not talked to them since day three when I smiled like an idiot. Help me do this without fake fire code.

### Assistant
You don't need a code citation to want a hallway for walking.

Talk to 4C again, in person if you can, with a date in the sentence. "The dresser is still out here. I need it gone by tomorrow. Groceries barely fit." Smile-from-day-three is cancelled. Neutral face.

If they say another weekend, you don't accept an open weekend. "Tomorrow, or I'll ask the building to have it moved." Only say the building part if you will actually email. Empty threats train dressers to stay.

I will not invent fire-code numbers. Hallways are for getting out. A dresser against a railing is worth a photo from your door, dated, in case you do write the office. Photo is not a declaration of war. It's a Tuesday.

Don't drag their furniture yourself. That's how you own a broken drawer.

Is the building staffed, or is this a no-desk walk-up where the landlord is a phone number?

### User
Phone-number landlord. Slow. There's a group email that nobody uses except for packages. 4C works nights. I hear them at 8 a.m. when I'm leaving. The dresser has a lamp on it now. That's what got me. It's becoming a room.

### Assistant
Lamp is the line. That's not storage. That's a hallway annex.

Catch them at 8 a.m. with the one-day deadline. Night workers aren't villains. They're just on a different clock. You still get the corridor.

If you miss them, a note on the dresser is allowed: date, your unit, "please move this inside today, it's blocking the hall." Tape that doesn't wreck the finish. Photo of the note plus the lamp.

Group email: one message, facts, if 4C doesn't move it after you asked. "Dresser and lamp in the 4th floor hall since [date], 4C said temporary, still there, path is tight." No novel. Landlord slow is why the email exists as a parallel track, not instead of talking.

Becoming a room is how people stop seeing it. You seeing it is useful. Keep seeing it until it's gone.

### User
What if they say they're getting a dolly on Friday and I should be patient because moving is hard.

### Assistant
Moving is hard. Eleven days plus a lamp is also hard for everyone who walks.

"Friday is too far. I need it out of the hall now. If you need a pair of hands for ten minutes tonight or at 8, say so. Otherwise it has to go back inside." Offering ten minutes of lift is optional kindness, not a requirement. Don't offer if your back is a problem. Don't become their mover.

If Friday is actually the soonest a dolly exists, they can put the dresser inside their unit until then. Inside is the point. Hallway is not a loading bay with a lamp.

No fake statutes. Photo, 8 a.m. sentence, note, email if they stall. Sideways groceries are a sufficient reason. Hall monitor is a nickname people use to keep furniture in public. You can live without the nickname and still want a path.

If they put the lamp back on it after a "soon," you photograph that too. A lamp is the annex flag. Tomorrow is still the word. Hands tonight only if your back allows and they actually move it inside, not further down the hall.

Hall monitor is a nickname. Path is a hallway. Eleven days plus a lamp is enough data. Talk, note, email, no dragging their dresser like you own the breakage. Sideways groceries were already the brief. Keep it that boring and that firm.
