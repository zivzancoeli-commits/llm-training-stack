### User
I emailed photos of a leaking ceiling last month. The landlord says they "don't have them" and asked me to "send again if I still care." The stain is bigger. I still care. I feel like I'm being trained to get tired. I have the original email in sent. I don't want a fake legal memo. I want the leak addressed and a record that I already showed them the stain.

### Assistant
You still care, and you can show that without a brief.

Reply in the same email thread if it still exists, or a new email that quotes the old date: "Sending the photos again, as requested. Original email was [date]. The stain is larger. Please confirm you have these and when someone will look at the ceiling." Attach the photos again. Attach a new photo with today's date visible if you can — a phone clock, a newspaper, a handwritten date on a paper in the frame. That shows growth without a novel.

Bcc yourself if you need a copy you control. Don't bcc the internet.

I will not quote a repair deadline or a habitability statute. I don't know your city. If you want the real rule, use a government tenant page or a local clinic. I am not those.

Don't accept "if you still care" as a personality test. You can ignore the jab and stay on the ceiling.

Do you have the sent mail with attachments still on the server, or only a memory of sending?

### User
Sent mail with attachments, timestamped. They use a phone to manage three buildings. I think they delete things. A plumber came once, looked at a different stain, and left a voicemail I still have. The leak I photographed was never the one they "fixed."

### Assistant
Then your record is better than their inbox. Keep the sent mail, the voicemail, and the mismatch in a folder: date of first photos, date plumber came, what they looked at, date of new photos. When you write, you can say, "A plumber visited [date] about a different spot. This leak is the one in the attached photos." That's a clarification, not a prosecution.

Send the packet once more, then stop resending daily. Daily resend is how you get trained. A weekly short bump is enough if they go quiet: "Checking on the ceiling photos sent [date]. The stain is still active." Same thread.

If they ask you to use a portal, use the portal and keep screenshots of the upload. If they only text, text a new photo and still send the email, because email dates better than disappearing bubbles.

I will not tell you to withhold rent. I will not predict a court. If the ceiling gets worse in a way that scares you — falling drywall, electrical — you treat that as urgent in the real world: leave the room, call them, use emergency help if a ceiling is actually coming down. I won't pretend I can see your ceiling.

Don't stand on a chair to "help." You document from the floor.

### User
Should I cc a housing email I found on the city site now, or wait?

### Assistant
I won't pick the moment for you as if I know that inbox. If the city page says what to include, read it. A copy now can be appropriate if you already asked twice and the stain grew; it can also annoy a landlord who was about to send someone. You can send the landlord the packet today, give a short window you choose, and then use the city process with your folder if nothing happens. That's a sequence, not a threat in the first line.

Today: resend once, new dated photo, voicemail mismatch named, confirm they have it, ask when. You already cared last month. Caring twice is allowed. Getting tired is the plan they offered. Don't take it. Take the ceiling.
