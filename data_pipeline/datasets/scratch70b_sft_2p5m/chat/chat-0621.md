### User
There is a greasy cassette on a towel where I eat. Chain bits. A cone wrench. Sam said the bike room is cold and the kitchen has "good light." I cook here. I don't want a workshop. I don't want a house meeting with snacks. How do I get metal off the table without a speech about respect?

### Assistant
The table is for plates. Good light is a lamp in the other room or a daytime floor. You do not need a meeting. You need a surface rule. Grease and lettuce should not share a commute.

Say: "Bike parts off the kitchen table. Use the mat in the hall or the bike room. I prep food here." If the cassette is there now, you can add, "I need this tonight. Please move it before 6." A clock beats a vibe. I will not invent a cleanliness law. I will not invent what grease does to wood. You already do not want it near lettuce. That is enough.

Do not stack their wrenches in a passive-aggressive sculpture. Move nothing that is not about to stain dinner if you can wait until they get home. If dinner is now and they are gone, put the towel-and-parts on the hall mat as one bundle. Photograph if you think they will say you lost a bearing. Then text: "Moved to the hall. Table is food."

Is the towel actually containing the grease, or is the wood already spotted?

### User
There's a dark comma on the table. They'll say it was already there. I have a sponge and I am angry at a comma. They get back at 7. I eat at 6:30.

### Assistant
Wipe the comma. Eat at 6:30. Bundle the parts to the hall before you cook so you are not sautéing next to a cassette. Text now, not at 7: "I need the table. I moved the bike stuff to the hall mat. Please keep parts out of the kitchen. There's a mark I'm wiping." You do not need them to confess the comma. You need the future cassette to live elsewhere.

If they say the bike room is cold, they can wear a jacket or wait for weekend daylight. Cold is not a lease on your cutting board. If they want good light, a clamp lamp is their purchase. I will not price it. I will not design their workstand. I will tell you the kitchen is not a stand.

Anger at a comma is allowed in your chest. It does not need to be a speech at 7. The text is the speech. 7 can be "thanks for moving it next time" if they bring it back in. If they bring it back in, you repeat the rule once. Repeat is not a meeting.

### User
They'll call me precious about a table we both paid for. We did both pay. That's the hook.

### Assistant
Joint rent is not joint workshop. You can say, "We both paid. That's why it's a kitchen, not a bench." Shared payment cuts both ways. They do not get extra because grease is a hobby. You do not get extra because salad is a hobby. The table has a job. Food.

If they need a project night, pick a not-table: hallway mat, balcony if you have one and it is safe, a board on the floor they vacuum after. Vacuum is their closing duty. You should not find grit by the toaster.

Tonight: bundle, wipe, cook, text. Precious is a word for people who liked the old mess. Let them use it in the hall. You use a plate. Cassette goes where chain oil can be ugly without being dinner. No snacks meeting. No respect sermon. A surface. A clock. A mat. Eat at 6:30. Let 7 come home to a clear table and a hallway full of bicycle. That is a fair split of light.
