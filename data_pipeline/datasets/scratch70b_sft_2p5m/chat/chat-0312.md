### User
The bathroom ceiling has a stain that grew. I texted my landlord ten days ago "please send the repair plan in writing" after they said a guy would come "soon." They replied "sure." Nothing in writing, no guy. Today they texted "can we swing by in 20 to look." I'm at work. I feel like if I say no I'm blocking the leak and if I say yes I lose the paper trail I asked for.

### Assistant
You can refuse a 20-minute swing-by while you are at work without refusing the repair. Those are different. "I'm not home. I still need the visit scheduled in writing — day, time window, who is coming. The stain is in the bathroom. I'll send a photo." That is cooperative and still a record.

"Sure" was not a plan. Forgetting is a pattern or a dodge. You do not need to decide which to keep documenting.

I will not tell you what your local housing rules require, or a magic number of days. I do not have your lease. I do have: photos with dates, a single thread, and not deleting "sure."

Are you able to leave work, and is the leak actively dripping or still a stain?

### User
I can't leave. It's a stain plus a smell after showers. Not a drip into a bucket. I have photos from day 1 and day 10. They hate email. They live on text.

### Assistant
Text can be writing. Forwarding to your email or screenshotting the thread into a folder is how you keep it. You do not have to win the medium. You have to keep the words.

Send the photo pair today with dates in the caption. Ask for a window that is not 20 minutes from now. Offer two slots you can actually be home, or ask whether they can use a spare key you already authorized in the lease. I do not know if you authorized a key. Do not invent permission you did not give. If they do not have a key, they wait.

Smell after showers is useful detail, not drama. Put it in the same text. Do not pile on theories about mold as a diagnosis. You are not a lab. You can say "the stain grew and it smells after hot water."

If they show up anyway while you are gone, that is a different problem. If you did not authorize entry, say so in the same thread: "Please don't enter if I'm not there unless we already agreed." Then read your lease tonight, not folklore.

If they get snippy about "in writing," you can say, "A text with a day and time is enough." You are not asking for a notary.

### User
If I keep asking for writing they'll call me difficult and delay more. The stain will just get bigger.

### Assistant
They can call you difficult. A growing stain with dated photos is still the stronger story if you ever need a third party — a local tenant resource, a relative who helps you read the lease, whoever you already trust. I will not name a government office as if I know yours.

Delay is their move. Your move is not to skip the record to be likable. Likable did not produce a plumber.

For the next ten minutes: send the not-home plus photo plus two time windows text, forward the thread to your email, and do not leave work. If they call, you can let it go to voicemail and reply in text so the words stay. If they agree to a time, repeat it back in one line.

A leak that "sure" would fix is still a leak. Keep it in writing even if the writing is a text bubble. Be available on a real window. Do not sprint home for a 20-minute maybe. That trains the 20-minute maybe.
