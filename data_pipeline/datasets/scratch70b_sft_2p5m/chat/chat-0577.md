### User
Uncle found digitized tapes. He wants to host a "viewing" at my apartment because I have the biggest TV and I live central. It's four hours of school plays, a wedding, and someone fishing. He wants snacks, a Saturday, and me to invite the cousins. I work weekends. My apartment is small for a reunion. I don't hate the tapes. I hate being the theater. How do I not host without looking like I hate the family history.

### Assistant
You can like the tapes and not be the theater.

"I can't host. You can watch at your place or pick a weekend at someone who offered a living room. I'll come for an hour if I can." Host is labor: chairs, snacks, invites, your Saturday. Attendance is smaller. Offer attendance if you mean it. Don't offer an hour you will resent.

Biggest TV is not a duty. Central is not a duty. Those are compliments he turned into a venue.

I will not invent a rule about who must keep family archives. He found the files. He can plug in a laptop at his house.

If you want to help without hosting: "Send me the folder. I'll watch some and talk to you about it." That's history without a crowd on your carpet.

Has he already told cousins it's at yours?

### User
He told two cousins "we're doing it at [my name]'s, they have the screen." So if I say no I'm canceling their plan. I didn't agree. I said "oh wow" when he mentioned the files. Oh wow was not a Saturday.

### Assistant
Correct the venue in a group message if the cousins are already in one, or ask him to. "I can't host at my place. I didn't mean oh wow as a yes. Uncle, can you send a new spot or we skip a big viewing." You're not canceling their plan. You're refusing a plan that used you as furniture.

Oh wow is how families book rooms. You get to unbook.

If a cousin is disappointed, they can host. Screens exist in more than one living room. If nobody hosts, people can watch files on their own laptops. A reunion is optional. The fishing tape will survive.

Snacks at yours is a second job. Don't list menus. Don't offer to "just do drinks." Drinks is how four hours happen.

Weekend work: say it. "I work Saturday. I'm not free to run an event." Work is a complete reason. You don't need to hate history to have a shift.

### User
He said we might not all be around next year, like a guilt weather report.

### Assistant
People use next-year weather when they want a room. You can answer the fear without opening the door.

"If you want to watch soon, do it at yours this month. I'll try to come. I still can't host." If you will not come, don't promise. Empty promises are how you get a four-hour guilt tape in your calendar anyway.

You don't hate the family history. You hate being volunteered by an oh wow. Send the correction before more cousins buy train tickets. TV stays yours. Tapes can play elsewhere. That's a no that still lets fishing exist on a screen that isn't in your apartment.

### User
I sent the correction. A cousin offered their basement. Uncle said basement TVs are "not the same." He still wants mine. I work Saturday. Oh wow is still haunting me.

### Assistant
Basement is a yes from someone else. Not-the-same is snobbery about your screen. You can like the tapes, skip the snobbery, and keep Saturday.

"Cousin's basement works. I work. I'll come for an hour if I can." If you can't, say you can't. Don't keep your apartment on the table as a backup venue. Backup venue is how oh wow returns.

Next-year weather can meet a basement this month. Files can be copied. History doesn't require your snacks. Invite list is uncle's if he still wants a crowd. You unbooked. Stay unbooked. Watch a fishing clip later on a laptop if you want the feeling without the reunion. That's not hate. That's a TV that isn't a theater this weekend.
