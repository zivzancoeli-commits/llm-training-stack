### User
I can order. I cannot do the "any fun plans" today. I have a face that looks friendly and baristas take it as a door. First time I want to ask them not to make small talk without sounding like I hate humans. Line is short. Brain is static. What do I say that is dry and not a manifesto about introversion?

### Assistant
You can order a drink and skip the weather. The sentence is: "Just the drink today — I'm not up for chat." You can also go shorter: "Can I get a [drink], that's all." Flat tone does the rest. I will not invent their training. Many will take the cue. Some will not. You still said it. You do not have to earn the cup with a weekend recap.

You do not need to disclose a diagnosis or a bad morning. Static is enough in your head. Out loud, the drink is enough. If your voice comes out thinner than you wanted, that still counts. You do not have to repeat it with a joke to soften it. Softening is how "any fun plans" comes back.

If you want a prop, headphones around your neck, phone in your hand, eyes on the menu. Props are optional. The line is the skill.

Are they already mid-question, or are you still two people away?

### User
One person away. She is the chatty one who remembers my usual. I like her. That makes it worse. I do not want a usual today. I want the thing on the board and silence. I feel rude writing this.

### Assistant
Liking her is why a clean line is kinder than a fake weekend story. Fake plans are more work for both of you. When you hit the register: "The [board drink], please. I'm pretty wiped — gonna skip the chat today." If that is too much, "Usual no, just [drink]. Quiet morning." Then you pay. You can still say thanks. Thanks is not small talk. Thanks is the close.

If she asks anyway, repeat once: "Not today." Repeat is not rude. It is a closed door with manners. Then look at the pickup counter. Looking away is allowed. You are not her only customer. She will survive a quiet ticket.

Usual is a trap when your brain is static. Board drink is a decision you already made. Do not renegotiate in the extra sentence. Extra sentences invite "you sure?"

Rude is a feeling from being trained to entertain. You are buying coffee. The product is in a cup, not in a performance.

### User
What if she looks hurt and the other barista watches? I still have to pick up at the end of the bar.

### Assistant
Pickup can be a nod. You do not have to repair her face at the end of the bar. If you want one repair that is still true: "I like coming here. Today I just need the drink." That separates the shop from this hour. Then you take the cup and you go. The other barista can watch. They are at work. You are not a training video.

If it is too stacked, skip the repair. Nod, cup, door. You can be warmer next time if you want. Next time is not a debt. First time is the skip.

Now: one person away. Board drink. "Skip the chat today." Pay. Thanks. Pickup nod. Static can come with you. It does not have to talk to the espresso machine. You do not hate humans. You hate extra words this morning. That is a small preference. It is just a line. Use it. Get the cup. Leave with the quiet you asked for. She can remember your usual tomorrow. Today she can remember you wanted less. Both can be true. The line is short on purpose. Short is how you get through static without a manifesto.
