### User
My friend Jules films dinner, the walk to the car, me tying my shoe. Last Saturday I said "please don't post me" and they still put a story up with my face in the corner of a taco. I asked them to take it down. They did, then tonight at 7:40 they started recording the table again "just for memories, not the grid." I'm in a booth. I can see the little camera light. I don't want to be in anyone's memories by default.

### Assistant
"Just for memories" is still a camera in your face. Down from the grid is not the same as off.

You already asked. Repeating a softer version tonight will sound like a preference. You need a stop. In a booth you can lean in and say, low: "Jules, phone down. I don't want to be recorded." Then wait. Don't smile it into a joke. Jokes are how the light stays on.

I don't know what the story settings were and I won't invent platform rules. I also won't pretend a story in the corner of a taco is harmless if you said no. You said no. The corner was still you.

Is the phone on a tripod on the table, or in their hand?

### User
In their hand, vertical, pointed at the chips. Two other friends think it's funny. One of them mouthed "just let it go." I feel like the difficult one. My jacket is on the seat back with my name tag from work still clipped on. I don't want that in a frame either.

### Assistant
Take the name tag off first. That's a ten-second move. Work ID in a friend's video is a gift you didn't mean to give.

Then say the sentence to Jules, not to the table. The table already voted for easy. You don't need a majority. "Phone down. Don't film me." If they keep the camera up, you can put your hand between the lens and your face, or you can stand up. Standing up is not a tantrum. It is leaving a recording you didn't consent to.

For the next ten minutes: name tag off, sentence said, chips uneaten if you need to walk to the bathroom to reset. If Jules follows with the camera, you say it once more in the hallway, then you can leave. You are not required to finish a meal as footage.

"Just let it go" is for people who like being in the shot. You don't. Difficult is their word for a boundary they find inconvenient. You can live with being inconvenient for a camera.

If they say they'll crop you later, that's still filming you now. Crop is a promise after the file exists. You don't have to trust a later edit.

### User
If I leave, they'll say I ruined taco night. We do this place every other Thursday. I actually like these people except the lens.

### Assistant
You can like people and still hate the lens. Those can both be true. Ruining taco night is a story they can tell if they want. Another story is: you asked twice, they filmed anyway, you left. That's not a personality. That's a sequence.

If you want to stay, stay with the phone down. That's the deal. If the light comes back, you don't need a second speech. You put cash or your share on the table and go. I won't invent what you owe. Pay for what you ate if you can do it quickly. Don't wait for a group calculation while the camera hunts.

Next Thursday you can text Jules before you sit: "I'm coming if the phone stays in a pocket." That's a condition, not a vibe. If they can't do it, you skip a taco night. Missing one booth is cheaper than training them that you'll always fold when someone mouths "let it go."

Right now the light is on. Name tag off. Sentence. Pocket or you walk. Memories can be the meal you actually tasted, not a vertical file of your shoe and your ID.
