### User
Holiday table. Relative asked when we're "starting" and then whether we'd "tried the thing her coworker tried." I am not discussing my body, my partner's body, or anyone's timeline. I have always deflected with a joke. The joke is used up. They are smiling, waiting. First time I want a clean close. I don't want a scene and I don't want to volunteer a medical story I don't have.

### Assistant
You don't owe a medical story. You don't owe a timeline.

"We're not talking about that. How's the garden / job / dog." Close, then a steering wheel. If you don't have a steering wheel, "I'm going to grab water" is a body exit. Jokes invited a sequel. Clean close does not.

If they say they're just excited, "I hear you. I'm still not discussing it." Repeat, don't expand. Expansion is how coworker-tips get on the table.

I will not diagnose you. I will not guess whether you're trying, not trying, grieving, or private for no reason they get to grade. Privacy doesn't require a reason.

Smiling wait is pressure. You can smile without answering. You can also not smile. Neutral is allowed at a table.

Is this one relative, or a chorus that piles on once someone opens the door?

### User
One relative, but my parent is watching to see if I'll be "nice." Partner is in the kitchen on purpose. If I shut it down, parent will do the talk in the car about family being family. If I don't, I'll be in the car mad at myself. I already said "we'll see" which they took as a window.

### Assistant
"We'll see" is a window. Nail a board over it now.

"I shouldn't have said we'll see. I'm not discussing fertility. Please stop." Please is optional. Stop is not. Nice is a car story. You can be polite in tone and still close the topic. Polite is not an interview.

Partner in the kitchen: you don't have to handle this as a duo unless you want a rescue. A rescue can look like partner returning with "we're not doing that conversation." If they're hiding, you still get to close it solo. You can tell them later you needed a hallway. That's a separate talk, without blame if they were drowning too.

Parent's car: you can pre-empt. "If this comes up again I'm going to shut it down. I'm telling you so the car isn't a surprise." Or you say nothing and survive the car. Both are valid. Don't let the future car write the present table.

Coworker tips: "No advice, thanks." Then water.

### User
They asked if something is wrong with me. That's the sentence that made my hands cold.

### Assistant
That's a probe dressed as care. You don't answer with a symptom list.

"Nothing I'm going to discuss. Drop it." If they go to "we're family," "Family can drop it too." Then you stand. Sitting is how the next question lands.

Hands cold is a reason to leave the chair, not to explain the chair. Bathroom, dishes, dog, porch. You don't need a diagnosis to refuse a diagnosis.

First clean close: no joke, no we'll-see, no coworker protocol, no car-prepayment that sells your no. Table gets a closed door. Relative can like gardens. You can keep your body off the menu. That's not a scene unless they make one. If they do, you already stood up.

If they follow you to the kitchen, the same two sentences, then dishes or porch. Partner can come back when they're ready; you don't have to scold the hiding, and they don't have to make a speech they didn't have. Parent's car can get "I shut it down on purpose." Family being family is not a medical interview.

Hands can warm up off the table. You didn't volunteer a story. You didn't joke. You didn't leave a we'll-see window. That's a first clean close. Garden, job, dog, water. Fertility stays off the menu. Sit somewhere else if you need to. You're allowed.
