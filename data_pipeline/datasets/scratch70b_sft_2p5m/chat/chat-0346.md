### User
My roommate starts a blender at 5:50 every weekday. Full smoothie, thirty seconds that feel like a jet. My bedroom shares a wall with the kitchen. I go to sleep around midnight. I have asked, once, if they could blend later. They said they have to leave for the gym at 6:10. I have been putting a pillow over my head like a cartoon. I need this to change without a roommate explosion. What is a reasonable ask, and what is the actual script?

### Assistant
A blender at 5:50 against a shared wall is not a personality. It is an appliance choice.

Reasonable asks, in order of least drama: blend the night before and fridge it; use a shaker bottle in the morning; move the blender to a counter that is not on your wall if that exists; close the kitchen door if there is one; a cheaper blender is not always quieter, so do not make "buy a new machine" the first demand.

Script, daytime, not at 5:51: "The 5:50 blend wakes me through the wall. I need mornings quieter. Can you mix the night before, or use a shaker before 7?" Offer the gym schedule a yes: they can still leave at 6:10 with a bottle that already exists.

Do not start with "you're inconsiderate." Start with the clock and the wall. If they need protein at dawn, protein does not legally require a motor.

Do they already meal-prep anything else, or is the smoothie the whole identity of 5:50?

### User
They prep chicken on Sundays. The smoothie is a frozen banana, powder, and ice. Ice is the scream. They said "I can try to be quicker," which is not quieter. There's no kitchen door. It's an open pass-through.

### Assistant
Quicker is still a jet. Ice is the problem. Frozen banana plus ice is a choice. They can skip ice if the banana is frozen. They can blend at 9 p.m. They can dump powder in a bottle with milk and shake. Those are all gym-compatible.

Stay on ice and hour, not on their fitness. "The ice in the blender at 5:50 is the noise. Night-before blend, or no blender before 7." If they want a concession, 6:45 on weekdays is a possible middle only if you can live with it. Do not offer 6:45 if you cannot. Fake compromise is how you get angry in March.

Put it in the house chat after you talk so it is not a fog. One message. No emoji pile.

If they refuse every option, you have a roommate conflict, not a smoothie misunderstanding. Then you talk about earplugs as your backup, and about whether this apartment's open kitchen can handle a 5:50 motor at all. Earplugs are for you. They are not permission for the jet.

You can also close your door, run a fan, and still ask. You should not have to soundproof a rental because of ice.

### User
They just texted "gym week starts tomorrow, I'll keep it short." Tomorrow is 5:50 again. Should I answer tonight?

### Assistant
Yes. Tonight, before you sleep.

"Short still wakes me. Please blend tonight or use a shaker in the morning. I need no blender before 7 on weekdays." Then go to bed. If the blender happens anyway at 5:50, do not start a fight in pajamas. Note the time. Talk again after work with the same sentence. Patterns need two data points and the same ask.

If you share a grocery list, stop buying bags of ice that only exist for dawn. That is petty-useful, not a war.

You are not killing their gym. You are moving a motor off a wall. Chicken already waits in a fridge from Sunday. A banana can too. Send the text. Put the phone down. If they blend at 5:50 anyway, you will handle it at 7 p.m., not at 5:51 with a pillow. That is the adult version of the cartoon.
