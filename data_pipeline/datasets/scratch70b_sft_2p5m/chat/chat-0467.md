### User
I'm in the car with my mom. She's driving. She asked if I'm "seeing anyone" and then named two people from high school like a menu. I usually give a joke and a maybe. I'm 28. I don't want to talk about who I date with her. This is the first time I want to say that without a fight on the highway. She's already smiling like she caught me. We're twenty minutes from her house. I have no other ride. Script that doesn't make this the rest of the weekend.

### Assistant
Short, then a new topic. Don't build a manifesto at 65 miles an hour.

"I'm not going to talk about who I date. It's not a secret emergency. I just don't want it as a topic. How's the garden / how's Aunt Lila / how's the thing she actually likes." You owe a redirect so the car has a place to go. If you only say the no, she'll poke the hole. Give her a hole that's allowed.

Don't joke-maybe. That's last year's maybe. Don't invent a fake breakup or a fake person. Don't say "I'll tell you when it's serious" if you don't mean that as a future leak. If you never want this topic, don't rent her a later.

If she says "I'm your mother," you can say, "I know. I'm still not discussing it." Then the garden. If she goes silent, let the radio exist. Silence on a highway is not a crash. Filling it with a name is how you lose the first time.

I won't tell you she has a right to know. I won't tell you you have to share. I don't know your family. You want a closed topic. That's the job.

Is anyone else in the car, and is this a weekend you can walk to a store if you need air?

### User
Just us. Weekend at her house. I can walk to a CVS. I tried the garden. She said "so that's a yes." She thinks the no is a confession. I can feel the old joke in my throat.

### Assistant
Don't swallow a name to disprove her. The joke is a name with a hat.

"It's not a yes. It's a boundary. I'm not doing dating talk this weekend. Tell me about the tomatoes." If she keeps fishing, you can put in earbuds for a song if that's not nuclear in your family, or you look at the window. You don't have to play courtroom. "So that's a yes" is a trick. You don't have to answer tricks.

When you get to the house, you can repeat once to set the weekend: "If it comes up at dinner, I'm going to change the subject. I'm not mad. I'm just not doing it." Then you help with bags. Movement.

If she tells your aunt anyway, you don't have to correct a rumor with facts. You can say to the aunt, "I'm not discussing that," and take plates to the kitchen. You don't owe a press conference because mom inferred.

CVS is allowed. A walk for toothpaste is a pressure valve. You can text a friend from the candy aisle. You still come back. You're not escaping the family. You're taking ten minutes.

### User
We're in the driveway. She turned the car off and is looking at me. This is the pause. I have to get the duffel from the back seat.

### Assistant
Get the duffel. Don't sit in the parked car for a bonus interview.

"I'm going to unpack. Not talking about dating. I'll make tea." Then you open the door. If she stays in the driver's seat for a feeling, she can. You go inside with the bag.

First time doesn't have to convert her. It has to be true in the driveway so the weekend has a rule. If she brings it up at dinner, you change the subject the way you said. You don't owe a highway fight. You owe yourself a closed topic and a cup of tea. Get the bag. The menu of high school names can stay in the car. You already left it. Walk into the house.
