### User
It's 12:05. I stood up. Raj pinged "got five min to sync?" I know his five minutes. I have a container of leftovers and a meeting at 1 that I actually need a brain for. He always catches me at the elevator. Last year I ate at my desk while he talked. I don't want to do that again. He's walking over. Script, please, that isn't "I have a doctor's appointment."

### Assistant
You can keep lunch. You don't need a medical alibi.

"I can't sync now — I'm going to eat. I can do 12:40 at your desk or a 10-minute Slack after 1." That's a no plus two real alternatives. If you don't have 12:40, don't offer it. "Tomorrow 9:15" is fine. Empty alternatives are just last year's dentist.

If he says it's urgent, ask the one question that sorts urgency from habit: "Is someone blocked this hour?" If yes, you can take five standing up, then you still eat. If no, you walk.

Don't hold the leftovers like a prop while you get pulled into a hallway. Movement toward the kitchen or the door is part of the sentence.

I won't invent whether your workplace "owes" you lunch. I don't know your labor rules. I will tell you that a 1 p.m. meeting is a reason to eat. That's enough.

Is this a peer, and do you already know what the sync is about?

### User
Peer. It's probably the same spreadsheet as yesterday. He likes to think out loud. He's now at my chair. The container is in my bag. I can smell the rice. I feel rude.

### Assistant
Rude would be letting him think out loud into your only pause. You standing up is not a crime.

"I'm heading out to eat. Ping me the cell that's stuck and I'll look after lunch." If he starts anyway, you pick up the bag. "I have to go. After 1." Then you leave the floor if that's what it takes. You don't sit back down "for a second." Sitting is how 5:50 happens at 12:10.

If he follows you to the kitchen, you can let him talk while you microwave, then you sit somewhere he doesn't. You are not obligated to provide a table and an audience. If that's too sharp for your culture, you can eat at a different floor. That's allowed.

The rice smell is not a personality flaw. It's lunch. He can smell it and live.

### User
He said "cool, I'll just walk with you." That's the trap. How do I eat without a mobile meeting?

### Assistant
"I need this one without work talk — my 1 o'clock is heavy. I'll find you at 12:40 or after the meeting." Then you don't add a joke to make it sweet. If he looks offended, he can eat with someone who wants a walking spreadsheet.

If he keeps talking anyway, you can put in headphones even if nothing is playing. That's a visual no. Last year's you would have performed interest. This year's you can chew.

Send the 12:40 or after-1 option once you're sitting, so you didn't lie. Then mute Slack until the container is empty.

If he sits down at your table anyway, you can eat without answering spreadsheet questions. "After 1" is still the calendar. Chew. If he waits you out, you can put the container lid back on and take it to a different floor. That's allowed. You're not trapping him; you're keeping the only pause you have.

If Slack pings "quick sync?" while you're chewing, you don't owe a reply until the fork is down. A delayed "after 1" is still a yes to work. It just isn't a yes to skipping food.

Next five minutes: bag, sentence, elevator, food. You don't skip lunch to sync. You sync at a time that isn't your only calories. That's the whole peer-management trick, and it never needed a fake appointment. The leftover rice can be lunch. The spreadsheet can wait until you have blood sugar. Both can be true in the same building.
