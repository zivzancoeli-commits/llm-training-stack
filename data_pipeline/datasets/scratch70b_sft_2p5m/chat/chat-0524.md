### User
I'm in a lobby. The show starts in twenty minutes. My tickets are in an app that is spinning on the QR. I screenshotted a blurry code an hour ago that looks like soup. The email confirmation is a PDF that says "present this" and then a blank rectangle. Wifi here is a rumor. I paid. I don't want to argue with a teenager at the door like I invented a ticket. Walk me through the next twenty minutes without fake refund rules.

### Assistant
You need a human with a list, not a prettier spinner.

Get off the venue wifi if that's what the app is choking on. Use cellular. Force-close the app, open it again, don't screenshot soup a second time as if that will sharpen. If the PDF is blank, don't keep pinching it. That's not a code.

Find the box office or will-call, not only the queue with scanners. Say: name on the order, email, last four of the card if they ask, show time. Ask them to look you up on their list. Many doors can search a name when a QR dies. I will not promise this one can. I will say a list is why you paid with a name.

If a teenager at a rope is the only person, you still use the same facts. You don't accuse them of incompetence. You ask for a supervisor or the box. Attitude wastes minutes you don't have.

I will not invent refund policy if you miss it. I don't know the vendor. Don't spend minute twelve writing a novel to customer service. Do that after you are in or after you are definitely out.

Do you have the order number in email, or only the spinning app?

### User
Order number in email. The app finally showed a QR for a second and then "session expired." My friend is already inside because theirs loaded. They're texting me to hurry. The line behind me is giving me looks.

### Assistant
Step out of the scanner line if you are blocking it. Blocking makes you the problem instead of the QR. Go to box office / guest services with the order number on the screen, brightness up, airplane mode off, battery not at 2 percent. If battery is dying, ask your friend to stay put, not to come out and lose their seat unless they want to. Two people in a lobby doesn't fix a session.

Tell the staff the QR expired. That's a sentence they hear. Let them regenerate or write a pass. If they need to see the card you paid with, have it out.

Don't try to use your friend's ticket to "pull you in." That's how you create a second problem. Don't hold your soup screenshot as if it were a passport. Offer the order number first.

If they say they can't find you, spell the email. Typos eat tickets. If the name is a nickname, give the legal one on the card.

Friend texts: "At box office, order [number]. Don't hold a scan line for me." Short. Hurry energy from inside doesn't load a QR.

I will not tell you to sneak. I will not tell you a door person "has to" believe a screenshot.

### User
Ten minutes. They're still looking me up. I feel like a scammer in my own receipt.

### Assistant
Stand still and keep the email open. You are a person with an order number, not a scammer. If they find you, you go in and you don't recap the spinner to the usher. If they don't find you before curtain, you ask what they can do for a late seat or a later show. You take their actual offer. Then you fight the vendor in email tomorrow with timestamps, not with a teenager's scanner.

Twenty minutes was always a list search, not a refresh contest. Stay at the desk. Let your friend sit. You already paid. Make them look up the pay, not your anxiety. That's the whole lobby job.
