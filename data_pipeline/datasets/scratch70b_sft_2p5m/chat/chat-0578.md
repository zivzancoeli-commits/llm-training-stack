### User
Utility bill arrived. It's an estimate. I already disputed the last estimate because nobody came to read the meter. Ticket said a reader would come in a window. Window passed. Nobody came. New bill, another estimate, higher, they say, because of "seasonal." I don't know what I used. I have a photo of the actual meter from yesterday. I am off work in two hours. I need a call script and a paper trail. No invented tariffs. No fake dollar amounts.

### Assistant
Two hours: photo, account number, ticket number, then a human.

Photo of the meter should show the dials or the digital total, the meter ID if it's on the face, and a date stamp from your phone. Take one more in daylight before you call if yesterday's is dark.

Call the number on the bill, not a search ad. Say: you disputed estimate [ticket], a read was promised in [window], no one came, you have a current photo of meter [ID], you do not accept another estimate without a read or an explanation of how they used your photo. Ask them to log the photo, schedule a real read, and tell you whether they will reverse or true-up the estimated line. I will not invent whether they must. You ask. You write the name of the person and the time.

I will not invent seasonal math. If they say seasonal, ask what number they used as the last actual read. If they don't have an actual, that's your point.

Don't threaten a legal department you don't have. Don't invent a price you think is fair. Stick to missed window, photo, ticket.

Is the meter in a locked closet they need access to, or outside where they could have read it?

### User
Outside, behind a bush they used to push aside. I trimmed the bush after the last miss. I can see the face from the sidewalk now. That's why the photo is good. Chatbot offered a "self-read" upload that errored twice. I have screenshots of the error.

### Assistant
Self-read error screenshots go in the file. Outside plus trimmed bush kills the "we couldn't access" story if they try it. Say that once, without a garden tour.

If the phone tree dumps you to the chatbot, wait for agent, or use the after-hours voicemail if that's all you get, plus an email to the billing address on the bill with the photo attached. Same facts. Timestamp.

Self-read failing twice is part of the dispute: you tried their path. Ask them to accept the photo another way — email, portal message, in-person. If they require a form, get the form name.

Higher seasonal estimate: "Give me the last actual read date and value." If the last actual is old, you're paying a story. True-up after a real read is what you want described in plain language. If they can't describe it, ask for a supervisor. Once. Then you log that too.

Two hours is enough for one complete call plus an email backup even if they "send a link." Links die. Email with photo lives.

### User
If they say pay the estimate now or they shut it off, I will panic. I don't want a made-up shutoff rule from you.

### Assistant
I won't invent shutoff rules. Ask them what happens if you pay vs. if you wait on a true-up, and get that in the chat or a follow-up email. If you can pay without wrecking rent, some people pay and keep disputing so the lights stay boring. If you cannot, you say that and ask about a hold while the read is outstanding. Their answer is the one that matters. Your panic wants a rumor. Use their sentence, written.

After the call: folder with photo, errors, ticket, agent name. Calendar the promised read window. If that window misses again, you call the same day it misses, not two bills later.

Off work, bush trimmed, photo ready, no fake dollars, no amateur tariff. You want a human to look at a meter that is now visible from the sidewalk. That's a reasonable two hours. Dial the bill number. Keep the chatbot screenshots. Repeat the missed window until someone logs it.
