### User
Priya is filming a cooking thing for her page. She wants me in the frame to "be honest" when the sauce breaks. Last time I was honest off-camera and she cried in the car. Now she wants honesty as content. I don't want to dummy a roast. I don't want to lie that the chicken is great if it is dry. The camera is already on a banana tripod. Help.

### Assistant
On-camera honest and friend-honest are different jobs. She is hiring the first with no pay and a tripod. You can refuse the role. You can still like her food in private on a different night when nobody is harvesting a take.

Say: "I'll eat with you. I won't be the commenter on camera." If you stay in the room, sit out of the shot or behind the banana. If she pans, you can wave it off. You do not have to perform a critic. You do not have to perform a host either. Eating is enough.

If the sauce breaks, you are not required to name it for the algorithm. You can hand her a towel. Help is a towel. Content is a verdict.

I will not rate the chicken. I will not invent a recipe fix as if I am in the pan. If she asks for a real tip off-camera later, and you actually know one, you can give it. Live, in the lens, "be honest" means "give me a clip." Decline the clip.

Is she asking for a roast, or for you to say "yay" on cue?

### User
She said "don't fake-nice, people can tell." Also "but don't kill the vibe." So I am supposed to be true and also a sitcom. There are two other friends who will say everything is iconic. I am the one she looks at when the pan goes wrong.

### Assistant
That look is the job offer. Do not take it. When the pan goes wrong, look at the pan, not at her forehead, and say nothing, or say "need a bowl?" Need-a-bowl is logistics. Iconic is the other friends' brand. You do not have to out-iconic them or out-true them.

If she pauses the recording and asks what you really think, you can use a small true: "It's salty" or "It's dry" or "I don't love this one." Small true, then you stop. You do not stack a monologue she will replay at 2 a.m. Last time's car cry was a stack. This time you keep a sentence.

If she wants you to repeat the sentence on camera, no. Off-camera is the only place that sentence lives. On camera it becomes a product with her face next to your mouth.

You can leave if the tripod starts hunting you. "I'm going to the other room" is allowed at a friend's house when you are being cast.

### User
What if she posts anyway with me in the background making a face I don't remember?

### Assistant
Then you ask her to crop you before she posts. "Please don't post my face on this." If she already did, you ask her to take it down or blur. I will not invent platform rules. I will tell you to ask once, clearly. If she refuses, you have information about the page versus the friendship. You still do not have to film take two.

For the next hour: out of the shot, towel, small true only if she pauses, no roast, no iconic. If she asks you to hold the phone "just for this flip," that is still crew. You can say no to crew too. Eat if you want. Lie if you do not want to be the clip. The honest thing she actually needs might be a friend who will not turn a broken sauce into a bit. Be that. Let the other two say iconic. You can say you are full. Camera stays on the pan. You stay a person, not a comment section with a pulse.
