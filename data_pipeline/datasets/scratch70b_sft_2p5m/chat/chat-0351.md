### User
I don't drink much. That's true. What is also true is that I have become the default driver for a group of five. Friday they already assumed my car. Someone dropped a pin to a bar I did not pick. I like these people. I do not like being a free taxi with a curfew I didn't set. Last time I sat in a parking lot until 1:40 while they "had one more." I have Saturday morning plans. How do I stop this without becoming the boring friend?

### Assistant
You stop it by saying it before the pin, not in the parking lot at 1:40.

Tonight, in the chat, before anyone is already dressed: "I can drive until 11:30. After that I'm going home. If you want later, we should plan a ride." Time is clearer than "I don't want to be DD." A clock they can plan around.

You are not boring for having a morning. You are a person with a car and a limit. Boring is a story drinkers tell designated drivers to keep the engine running.

You can still go. You can still drink a soda. You can leave on time. Leaving on time is the boundary. Sitting in a lot is how they train you.

If they need a true all-night driver, they can rotate, pay a ride, or stay nearer transit. That is a group problem. It became yours because you are convenient and kind. Convenience is not a contract.

Did they already confirm you as driver in the thread, or is it only the pin so far?

### User
Someone wrote "usual car?" and three people liked it. I haven't replied. The bar is 25 minutes from my place. Two of them live in the other direction.

### Assistant
Reply now. "I can do a ride there and a ride back by 11:30. I'm not doing the late lot wait. If you're staying later, grab a ride from the bar." Then go or don't go on those terms.

If they like-react that with a party emoji, you still leave at 11:30. Reactions are not a rewrite.

Do not pick everyone up in a complicated loop that makes you the regional transit authority. If two live the other way, they can meet at the bar or share a car that is not yours. You can take the people on your side of town. Splitting is allowed.

Bring the thing you need for Saturday so you are not tempted to "just stay." Put it on the passenger seat. A visible morning is a reminder.

If they tease "come on, one more," you start the car or you hand them the address of a ride app and you leave. You can be warm about it. Warm and leaving is a skill. Warm and waiting is how 1:40 happens.

### User
I keep thinking if I stop driving they won't invite me. That sounds clingy even to me. I still think it.

### Assistant
It might change the pattern. People who wanted a taxi will invite the taxi less. People who wanted you will still text. You will find out which group this is. That information is useful. It is not clingy. It is data.

If they drop you, that hurts and it is still not a reason to sit in a dark lot. You can grieve a group and keep your Saturday.

Send the 11:30 message. Do not over-explain the class or the grocery run. A time is enough. Then decide if the bar is worth a 25-minute drive under that rule. If it isn't, skip this Friday. Skipping once teaches faster than a paragraph. You can be the friend who comes and leaves. That is a type of friend. It is a better type than the parked engine. Put Saturday's thing on the passenger seat before you leave the house so the clock has a witness. Then keep the time you wrote.
