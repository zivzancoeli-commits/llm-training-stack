### User
The freezer is a snow cave. I cannot pull the peas out without a chisel energy. Jordan never defrosts. They say ice is "just water" and that opening the door causes it. I defrosted last time and found a bag of fish from a year I did not live here. I don't want a chore chart novel. I want the freezer to open and I want not to be the only person with a towel on the floor.

### Assistant
Ice is water, and it is also a jammed drawer. You can skip the physics debate. You need a thaw day and a split of the work.

Say: "We need to defrost this weekend. I did the last one. You own this one, or we do it together on Saturday morning." Together is if you do not trust them to throw your peas. Own-it is if you want a day off. Pick. I will not invent a lease clause about frost. I will not invent a power-company trick. Unplug or use the defrost setting your unit actually has. Read the sticker inside if there is one.

Put a towel down. Bin the mystery fish if it is theirs and they agree. If they do not agree, it still does not get to block the peas. Photograph unlabeled meat before you toss if you want to avoid a fight. I will not tell you the food is safe. I will not date a bag from a year you did not live here as edible. When in doubt, out.

Do they admit the cave is a problem, or only the door-opening theory?

### User
They admit it is annoying and still did not pick a day. Saturday I work. Sunday I could. They have a brunch. The peas are for a dinner tomorrow. I can barely close the door now. It beeps.

### Assistant
Tomorrow's peas are the clock. You may need a tactical chisel tonight: run a little warm water on the drawer edge, not a knife into a coil. If you do not know where the coils are, do not stab. A plastic spatula and patience. If the bag rips, that is tonight's tax. I will not pretend I can see your freezer.

For the cave: Sunday they skip brunch or you skip nothing and they come home to a project you already started with their name on a text: "Sunday 10, freezer defrost, I need you here for the wet part." If they miss it, you still defrost because the beep will not wait for brunch, and then they owe the next two. Write that. Patterns need numbers.

If they say opening the door causes ice, you can say, "Then we defrost more often." You do not need to win meteorology. You need a towel and a trash bag.

Beep: close it as flush as you can tonight after you extract tomorrow's food. A jammed gasket is how kitchens smell. If it will not close, defrost cannot wait until Sunday. That is a tonight job. Say so.

### User
If I do it tonight they'll thank me and never take a turn. That's the whole dynamic. Also I don't want meltwater in the kitchen at 11 p.m.

### Assistant
Then do the minimum tonight: peas out, door as closed as it gets, towels if it is dripping, no full heroic thaw at 11. Text: "I got tomorrow's food out. Sunday 10 we defrost. If you're at brunch, change brunch. I am not doing the cave alone again." If they thank you in advance, do not accept the thanks as a swap for Sunday. Thanks is not a shift.

If Sunday they vanish, you have a choice: live with peas in a cooler bag for a week while the cave wins, or do it and then stop cooking their frozen stuff, stop buying shared ice cream, stop being the kitchen parent. Shrinking the shared freezer is a lever. I will not tell you to throw their fish in anger. I will tell you unlabeled year-old fish can go in the trash with a photo.

Tonight: no 11 p.m. lake, extract, text Sunday. Weekend: wet floor, two people, mystery meat gone. Just water can live in a drain. It does not get to be a glacier on your peas. Make Sunday a calendar, not a vibe. Then close the door until it beeps once and shuts up.
