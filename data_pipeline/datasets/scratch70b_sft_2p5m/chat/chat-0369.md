### User
My cousin set up a phone against the salt shaker, screen facing us, and said "don't mind me, it's just for memories." It is recording audio and video of the whole table, including two people who didn't hear the sentence. I don't want tonight on a camera roll. I also don't want to be the person who kills the vibe in the first ten minutes of a lasagna. What do I say, and do I have to speak for the people who didn't notice?

### Assistant
You can speak for your own face immediately. You can also flag the others.

To your cousin: "Please don't record the table. I'm not up for that." Hand-wave at the salt shaker. If they say it's only memories, "I don't want to be in it. Please stop and delete this one." Delete is part of the ask if they already have five minutes.

You do not need a privacy seminar. You need the red light off.

For the people who didn't hear: "FYI the phone is recording." Then they can choose. You are not their lawyer. You are not required to stay quiet so the lasagna stays smooth. Smooth lasagna with a secret microphone is not actually smooth.

If cousin gets huffy, let them. Huffy is cheaper than a file.

Is this a live story going to a family group, or a camera roll they swear they'll never post?

### User
They swore camera roll. Last Thanksgiving a clip of my other cousin talking with food in their mouth hit a chat. So the swear is weak. The two who didn't hear include my dad, who hates phones at meals and will explode if he finds out late.

### Assistant
Stop it before Dad finds out late. Late explosion is worse than a ten-minute awkward.

Repeat: "Phone down. Not recording dinner." If they hesitate, you can turn the phone face down yourself if it is in reach and that will not become a grab fight. If it would be a grab fight, you don't grab. You insist.

Tell Dad in one line if the recording already happened: "There was a phone up; I asked them to stop." He can explode at the cousin, not at you for hiding it.

Ask them to delete the file in front of you. Watching the trash icon is petty and reasonable given Thanksgiving. If they refuse, you know the swear is theater. Then you sit out of frame or you leave the chair. You do not have to perform for a roll.

Lasagna will survive. Vibe is a word people use to keep cameras running.

### User
They paused it and said "I'll just do audio." Audio is still the table.

### Assistant
"No audio either. Dinner isn't content." Same calm. Audio is how food-in-mouth becomes a chat clip without a face. Dad will not like that better.

If they pocket the phone, you are done. If they leave it recording in a pocket, you can say, "Off, please." Then eat.

You spoke. You flagged. You do not have to host a policy conference. The salt shaker can go back to being salt. If cousin wants memories, they can write a sentence in a notes app after pie. That has been a method for a long time. It does not require a mic on the ricotta. Keep the phone down. Pass the lasagna. That is a memory too, and it doesn't need a file.

### User
Dad just noticed the phone and asked "is that on?" I haven't answered. Cousin is looking at me.

### Assistant
"It was. I asked them to stop. No recording." Then look at the cousin, not at Dad's volume. You already told the truth. Dad can escalate if he wants. You do not have to pre-explode to prove loyalty.

If cousin says it is off, you can ask them to show the screen. That is the Thanksgiving lesson again. Then eat. You did the job in the first ten minutes, which is when it was still cheap. Let Dad and cousin finish the rest if they must. Your lasagna does not have to wait on a verdict. Phone down. Fork up. That is the table you wanted. You named the camera. That was enough of a speech.
