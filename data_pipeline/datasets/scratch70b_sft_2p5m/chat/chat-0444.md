### User
Nia and I had a fight Tuesday about me canceling a trip. Today she asked me to be her emergency contact at urgent care "because you're still the person." She wants my name on a clipboard in the next hour. I don't know if we're okay. I don't want her to sit there with a blank line. I also don't want to be legally or practically on the hook if I don't even know what she's being seen for. She's in the waiting room texting. What do I say that isn't a second fight and isn't a fake yes?

### Assistant
Emergency contact is a practical job, not a friendship trophy. You can ask what the job is before you sign a clipboard you can't see.

Text: "I need to know what you want me to do if they call. I'm not automatically okay being listed if we're still in the middle of Tuesday. I can still help you get through this visit another way." That's honest. If she's in pain, you don't need a relationship seminar in the first bubble. You can add, "Are you safe right now? What are they seeing you for, in one line?" I will not diagnose her. I won't guess the clinic's rules. You can still ask what a call would mean: ride, decision, or just a name they never dial.

If you are willing to be the person they call for a ride, say that. If you are not willing to make medical decisions, say that. Those can be split even if a form is crude.

Don't agree because you feel guilty about the trip. That's how you get a 2 a.m. call you resent, which becomes fight three.

Are you in the same city, and can you actually show up if they phone you?

### User
Same city, twenty minutes away. I could show up. I don't want to make treatment decisions. I don't even know if this is a sprain or something she's scared of. She said "don't make this about Tuesday." The clipboard lady is waiting. Nia says they need a name now.

### Assistant
Then give a name with a scope, out loud to Nia, not a blank check.

"You can put my name and number for them to call if they need a ride or if you're not answering. I'm not agreeing to decide treatment. If that's the job, pick someone else or ask them what the line means." She can ask the clipboard person, "Does emergency contact mean they ask this person to decide things, or just a phone number?" Let the clinic define their box. I won't invent it.

If she still wants you and you can do the ride, "Yes for pickup, no for decisions" is a sentence she can write in the margin if they'll let her. If they won't, you decide whether a crude form is still worth it. A twenty-minute radius ride is a real yes. A medical proxy is a different document. Don't mix them because a line is short.

Tuesday can wait until she's not in a vinyl chair. You can say, "We'll talk about the trip later. I'm answering this as a ride." That's not making it about Tuesday. It's refusing to let Tuesday force a fake forever.

If you don't want your name on it at all, you say that now, before the lady walks off. "I can't be the contact today. Call your sister." It will sting. A false contact stings later.

### User
She put me down anyway while I was typing. I didn't say yes. Do I go down there and make them change it? That feels huge.

### Assistant
You don't have to storm the desk if you're still willing to be the ride. You do have to reply to Nia: "If you already wrote me, I'm the ride, not the decision-maker. If they call, that's what I'll say." If you are not willing even to be the ride, then yes, you tell her to change it, and you can call the desk yourself if she won't: "I was listed without agreeing. Please take me off." That's not huge. That's accurate.

You didn't say yes. She used the silence. That's information about Tuesday too, but you don't have to try that case in urgent care.

Next hour: clarify the job in one text, go or don't based on whether you actually consent, don't expand into treatment. The clipboard is a form. You still get to define the friendship labor. Get her home if that's the yes. Leave the trip for a day when nobody is in a waiting room.
