### User
Train at 4:20. It's 1:18. Sitter texted they have a "family thing" and can't do the cat. Cat is 14, needs a pill at 8 p.m. and wet food. I'm going to my brother's for two nights. Ticket is already on my phone. I asked the sitter three weeks ago. There's a spare key in a plant pot they were supposed to use. I am packed. The cat is on the suitcase.

### Assistant
The cat on the suitcase is the true schedule. "Family thing" at 1:18 may be real. It is still a cancel. You don't need to cross-examine it. You need a pill at 8 and a body in the apartment, or you need to not get on the 4:20.

I'm not a vet. I won't tell you it's safe to skip the pill. A 14-year-old cat with a timed med is a reason to treat 8 p.m. as non-optional unless a vet already told you otherwise. I don't have that chart.

Three hours is ugly and it's enough to try two humans and one backup plan.

Who else has ever fed this cat — a neighbor, a coworker, the brother you're traveling to?

### User
Neighbor in 3A has fed her once. Brother is the trip. Coworker is at work until 5:30. There's a vet clinic I use but I don't think they board same day. Plant pot key is still in the pot. I haven't told my brother. He bought groceries for me.

### Assistant
Text 3A now: "Sitter cancelled. Can you do 8 p.m. pill and wet food tonight and tomorrow morning? Key still in the pot, I can show you the pill box in ten minutes." That's a specific job, not "watch my cat." If they can't do both nights, ask for tonight and you'll find morning later.

For the next ten minutes: message 3A, message one other person, call the clinic and ask if they board or know a sitter. Don't spend the ten minutes writing the sitter a review. That's for the platform later. I won't invent boarding availability.

Tell your brother the situation so the groceries don't become a guilt monument. "Sitter cancelled, I might miss 4:20. I'll update by 3." He can eat the groceries. You can still come tomorrow morning if tonight fails.

If 3A says yes, you show the pill, you write the dose on a sticky, you still make the train. Put a water fountain or extra water down. 14 years old. Don't get cute with new food.

If nobody says yes by 3:00, you change the ticket if you can, or you don't go. A two-night trip is not more important than the 8 p.m. pill unless you have a person. I'm not going to romanticize "she'll be fine." You don't know that.

### User
The sitter asked if they still get paid for "holding the dates." I haven't answered. 3A hasn't read the text.

### Assistant
Don't pay for a cancel at 1:18 unless some written policy you already agreed to says so. I won't invent the app's rules. "Holding the dates" is a sentence. Your answer can wait until the cat is covered. If you reply now, "I need to sort the cat first" is enough.

If 3A hasn't read, knock. A text to a quiet phone is not a plan. Knock, explain in two sentences, show the pill box. People say yes in hallways when they won't tap a notification.

You have until about 3:30 to still make a 4:20 with a bag. Use the hallway. The suitcase can wait by the door. The cat can get off it. If 3A is out, try the coworker for after 5:30 for tonight only and move your train to tomorrow. Brother's groceries will keep.

Hold the sitter's payment question in a drawer. Coverage first, money second, review third. 1:18 is for knocking. 4:20 is only real if 8 p.m. has a name next to it. Get the name. Then go or stay. The plant pot key is useless if nobody's hand is on it. Put a hand on it.
