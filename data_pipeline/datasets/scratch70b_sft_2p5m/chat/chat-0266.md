### User
I have a 5:45 with the same barber as last month. Last month they went too tight on the sides and I looked like I joined something I didn't join. I wore a hat for two weeks. I still tipped. Tonight I want the top left alone and the sides only a little. I have never said I hated a cut. I don't want them to take it as an insult. I also don't want another hat season. I'm on the bus. It's 5:22.

### Assistant
You don't have to say "I hated it." You have to say what to do this time. Hate is a review. Instructions are a map.

On the bus, pick three words you can repeat: "Leave the top. Sides a little. Last time was too tight." Too tight is a measurement. Insult is optional and unused.

You're allowed to switch barbers. You're also allowed to give this person a clear second chance. First time saying it is not a betrayal. Last month they couldn't read a hat.

I won't invent what a number 2 versus a 3 means in their shop. If you don't know numbers, skip numbers. Use fingers on your own head: "this much off, stop here."

Are you trying to keep this barber long-term, or is tonight a last test?

### User
Last test. They're two blocks from work. Convenience is why I stayed. There's another place further that has good photos. I already paid a deposit in the app for 5:45. If I walk in and freeze I'll get the same cut. I can feel the freeze coming.

### Assistant
Deposit means you show up or you eat the deposit. I won't guess the amount. Showing up with a sentence is cheaper than a freeze plus a hat.

For the next ten minutes on this bus: say the three sentences out loud once, quietly, like a grocery list. When you sit, say them before they pick up the clipper. Before. Clippers moving is how last month happened. You can add "please stop so I can see the side in the mirror before you go tighter." That's a checkpoint, not a fight.

If you freeze, hand them your phone with a photo of your hair at a length you liked, even a blurry one. Photos beat vibes. If you have no photo, use your hand.

Don't apologize for last month's hat. Don't tell a funny story about joining something. Humor is how they think you're over it and they repeat the tight sides.

If they get defensive, you still don't owe a soothe. "I just need it longer this time" is enough. If they start the same tight pass anyway, you can say stop. Stop is allowed mid-cut. Awkward. Better than two weeks of brim.

### User
What if they ask why I didn't say something last month when I tipped?

### Assistant
Because you froze, and because tipping is how you leave a chair, not a grade. You can say "I should have said it then. I'm saying it now." That's true. Don't debate the tip. The tip is spent.

If this is the last test and they ignore the checkpoint, you go to the further shop next time. Convenience is not a haircut. Two blocks is a walk. A good photo shop is a longer walk with a better head.

You're at 5:22. Get off, walk in, sit, speak before metal. You can be polite and specific at the same time. Polite is "please." Specific is "not that tight." The freeze wants you to nod. Nodding is last month. Use the grocery-list voice. Then look in the mirror when they pause. If they don't pause, you ask. That's the whole first time. Hats can stay on the hook at home. The bus can keep moving. You get off anyway. If your voice comes out smaller than you practiced, the three sentences still count. Repeat them. Clippers do not get to outrun a sentence you already paid a deposit to say.
