### User
It is 1 a.m. Someone upstairs is doing what sounds like moving a gym. I have work at seven. Last year I would have banged a broom on the ceiling and then hated myself. I texted you instead of the broom. I might just need to rant. I might want a script. I do not actually know.

### Assistant

If you still want a plan, tell me whether you already know these people, and whether this is a one-off party or a pattern. I will not guess your lease.

### User
Keep going. I still want help, I think. I have seen them in the stairwell twice. We nod. This has happened two other weeknights. Not a party. More like a person who does not believe sound travels down. I am in a T-shirt in the kitchen trying not to become last-year me.

### Assistant
Okay. We keep the broom in the closet.

Tonight, in the next few minutes: earplugs or headphones if you have them, a glass of water, and a decision that you are not knocking at 1 a.m. unless you smell smoke or hear someone in trouble. A 1 a.m. knock on a nodding acquaintance turns a noise problem into a fear problem. Last-year you wanted a feeling of hitting back. It rarely made the next night quieter.

Write one sentence in your notes app while you are still angry, so morning-you does not sand it into nothing: "Weeknight noise after midnight, third time, sounds like dropping or dragging." Time stamp it. That is for a daytime conversation or a note, not for a hallway trial tonight.

Then try to sleep in the least loud room if you have a choice. If you cannot sleep, do not rehearse the speech until 3 a.m. Rehearsing is how you arrive at their door already in a fight.

### User
Daytime then. I do not want to be the building cop. I also do not want to write a paragraph to the landlord as the first move. What do I actually say when I see them with their groceries.

### Assistant
Still want the script, or are you cooling off and you want me to stop? If you still want it:

Groceries, eye contact, normal volume. "Hey — I'm below you. A couple weeknights, including last night around one, there's been a lot of impact noise and I can't sleep. Could you keep the heavy stuff earlier, or on a rug?" Then pause. Let them talk. If they apologize, "Thanks. I work early" is enough. If they get defensive, do not escalate in the stairwell. "I'm just asking. I'll follow up in writing if it keeps happening" is a boundary, not a speech.

Do not diagnose their character. Do not mention the broom you did not use. Do not smile-lie that it is fine.

If you never see them, a short note under the door with the same content is the next tool. Landlord is for a pattern after you have asked once, or for a lease issue I will not invent. First move is the human you already nod at.

Last-year you wanted the ceiling to learn a lesson at 1 a.m. This-year you wants sleep and a job at seven. Tonight you protect sleep. Tomorrow you use the sentence you wrote down. If you decide in an hour that you only needed the rant, you can ignore the script. I am checking because you said you were not sure. You still in, or are we done and you are going back to bed? Either answer is allowed. I would rather you sleep than perform a perfect tenant.
