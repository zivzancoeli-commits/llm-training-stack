### User
I returned a jacket online on the 3rd. Tracking says they scanned it on the 7th. Today is the 22nd. The site chat is a bot that keeps offering store credit at a number I don't want. I want the original card back. There's no phone number that reaches a person. I used this jacket's money for rent math. I'm at the kitchen table with the return label photo. The bot just said "I understand your frustration" for the third time.

### Assistant
The bot understands nothing. That's a template. Store credit is their preference. Your preference is the card. Those can both exist. You don't have to accept the credit in the chat to be "reasonable."

I will not invent how many days a refund "must" take or what the law is. I don't know this seller or your card's chargeback rules. I can tell you a scan on the 7th plus silence to the 22nd is enough to escalate past a bot.

Don't argue with "I understand." Change channel.

Do you have an order number, the return RMA, and the tracking screenshot in one place, or is it split across email and the app?

### User
Order email, tracking screenshot, RMA in the app. I tried the "talk to agent" button. It looped. My card app shows the original charge still pending-nothing, just sitting as posted from last month. I don't want to do a chargeback if that's nuclear. I also don't want store credit I'll never spend.

### Assistant
Chargeback isn't always nuclear. It also isn't always the first move. I won't tell you to file one as a fact of life. It's a tool your card may offer after you try a human. If you don't know the card's process, you can read it without filing tonight.

For the next ten minutes: put order number, RMA, tracking, dates (shipped 3rd, scanned 7th, today 22nd) in one note. Email the address in the footer of the order, if there is one, with that block and "Please refund to original payment method, not store credit." Subject line with the order number. If they have a social support handle you already use, one public-brief or DM with the same block, not a rant.

Search the site footer for a phone even if chat hid it. Sometimes it's only on the packing slip. You have the label photo. Zoom.

If the bot offers a survey, skip it.

Don't click accept credit. Clicks are agreements you don't mean.

### User
If I email, they'll say 30 days. 22 already feels like they ate it. Rent math was not theoretical.

### Assistant
They might say 30. That's a sentence you can answer with "scanned on the 7th, please confirm refund to card by [a date you choose, soon]." I cannot promise they honor it. If rent is tight, that's a reason to open the card's dispute help article tonight and see what evidence they want, without hitting submit until you decide. Reading is not filing.

22 days is long enough to be angry. Anger doesn't type well in the bot. It types fine in a dated email.

Kitchen table: send the email, save the PDF of tracking, close the chat so you stop collecting fake empathy. Tomorrow, if nothing, try the phone on the slip or the card. Store credit can sit unclicked. The jacket is already their problem in a warehouse. Your problem is a line on a statement. Treat it like a paper trail, not a personality contest with a script.

You don't have to be polite to the bot. You should be factual to a human inbox. That's the split. Close the bubble that said frustration. Open the mail app. Attach the scan date. Ask for the card. Then get up from the table so you don't refresh a loop. The loop is designed to tire you into credit. Don't take the credit. Take the trail.
