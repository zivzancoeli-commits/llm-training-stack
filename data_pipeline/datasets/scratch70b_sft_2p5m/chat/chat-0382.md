### User
There's black stuff in the bathroom corner again. I texted photos two months ago. A guy came, wiped it, painted over it, and left. It smells like a basement after showers. The paint is bubbling. My landlord says "we already treated it." I don't want to play inspector. I also don't want to keep breathing this. I need a way to talk to them that isn't a rant and isn't me pretending the paint fixed it.

### Assistant
Paint is not a conversation-ender. It is a surface. You can stay on what you can see, smell, and photograph without becoming a mold scientist.

I will not diagnose what the black stuff is, and I will not invent a health outcome or a housing statute. I am not your inspector. What you can do is keep the record boring and keep the request specific.

Send a new message with today's photos next to the old ones. One paragraph: the corner returned, the paint is bubbling, the smell is back after showers, you want someone to look at the source, not another coat. Ask for a time window. Do not write a novel about how you feel in the mornings.

If they reply "already treated," you can answer: "The same corner is failing again. I need a repair visit, not a recap of the last one." That is not a rant.

Are you still in a lease with this landlord, and do you have the old photos and the date of the paint visit?

### User
Yes, lease runs through spring. I have the texts, timestamps, and a short video of the bubbling. They like to answer after 9 p.m. with "we'll keep an eye on it." I live here. I am the eye. I don't want to threaten anything. I also don't want another wipe-and-paint next Tuesday.

### Assistant
Then stop accepting "we'll keep an eye on it" as a plan. Eyes do not fix corners. You want a scheduled visit with a stated job: find why it returns after showers, not cover it.

Reply in the same thread so the history stays attached. "Please schedule someone to look at the bathroom corner this week. The paint is bubbling and the smell is back. I can be home Thursday after 5 or Saturday morning. I don't want another paint-only visit." Offer two times. People stall when the calendar is blank.

During the visit, be in the room. Point at the bubble, the corner, the fan if it is weak. Ask them to say out loud what they think is happening. You are not required to agree. You are required to hear the words. If they reach for a roller immediately, you can say, "I need the source checked before paint." If they refuse and only paint, photograph the visit too. Same thread after they leave: "Visit today was paint only. The smell and bubbling were not addressed. Please advise next step."

I will not tell you to withhold rent, break a lease, or cite a code. Those are legal moves I will not invent. If you want that lane, that is a local housing person or a tenant group, not a chat script. For this week, the job is a dated ask and a dated record.

Keep showers shorter if that reduces the steam you can control, run the fan the whole time plus after, and crack the door. That is not you accepting blame. That is you not adding fog while you wait.

### User
What do I say if they tell me I don't ventilate enough and it's my fault for taking hot showers?

### Assistant
You can acknowledge steam without taking the whole building on your back. "I run the fan. The corner still failed after the last paint job. I need the source checked." Then you return to scheduling. Do not debate whether you are a clean person. That debate is a stall.

If they want a humidity lecture, let them lecture for one minute and then repeat the visit request. Repeating is how you stay out of a personality fight.

If they never schedule, you still have the thread, the photos, and the dates. That packet is what you take to whoever you choose next: a different repair request, a city process if you use one, or advice from a local clinic or inspector if you decide you want an outside look. I will not promise what those offices will do.

Tonight: one text, two photos, two time windows, no threat. Tomorrow: do not re-explain in a second essay. If they reply late, answer the next morning in the same thread. Keep living in the bathroom like a person, not a defendant. The paint already had its turn.
