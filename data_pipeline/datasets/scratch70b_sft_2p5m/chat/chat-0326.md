### User
I had a procedure. I told my family I was having "something looked at" and that I am okay. My aunt keeps texting for the name of it, the doctor, what they found, and whether it is "the same thing Uncle had." I do not want to give her a file. She forwards things. I also do not want a war. I am tired and I am supposed to rest.

### Assistant
You do not owe her a chart. "I'm okay, I'm resting, I don't want to discuss details" is a full medical update for an aunt who forwards.

You already said you are okay. Repeating that is not a lie if it is true. If you are not okay, you still get to choose who knows the name of the thing. I will not guess the procedure, and I will not compare it to Uncle. I cannot. You should not let her diagnose by family folklore either.

Do not send a photo of a discharge paper to make her stop. That is how it ends up in a cousin thread.

If she keeps asking, you can slow the channel: "I'm not answering medical questions. I'll tell you if I need help with meals." Offer a practical substitute if you want help, not a lecture on privacy.

Who is the aunt close to that she might recruit — your parent — and have you already told a parent more than you told her?

### User
My mom knows more. Mom said "just give her something so she stops." Mom is tired of being the switchboard. The aunt is in a group text with three people I did not include.

### Assistant
Mom wants you to pay a detail tax so the switchboard shuts off. You can support Mom without paying it. A message to Mom: "Please don't share more than I did. If Aunt asks you, you can say I'm resting and not going into it." That is help for the switchboard. It is a script, not another secret.

To the aunt, still in a single text, not the group if you can help it: "I'm not discussing the medical stuff. Thanks for caring. I need quiet." If she posts in the group, you do not have to correct a rumor with the truth. You can ignore, or you can say "I'm fine, no more details" once. Feeding the group a name is how Uncle's thing gets attached to you.

If they guess wrong, you do not have to play quiz. Wrong guesses are their hobby.

I am not a lawyer. I will not frame this as HIPAA for a family text. I will say forwarding is why your instinct is good.

Rest is the assignment. Every extra explanation is unpaid work.

### User
If I go silent she'll show up. She has done that. I don't have the energy for a living room interview.

### Assistant
If showing up is real, you can pre-empt with Mom or with a locked door plan: you are resting, visits off, meals on the porch if someone must drop food. Text aunt: "Please don't come by. I'll reach out when I want company." If she comes anyway, you do not have to do the interview. You can speak through the door, or let Mom handle the stoop if Mom is willing. I do not know your house.

You can also have one canned sentence ready: "I'm not talking about the procedure. I need rest." Repeat. Become a broken record. Interviews die when they do not get new clips.

For the next ten minutes: send aunt the no-details-plus-quiet text, send Mom the switchboard script, silence the group or leave it, and put the phone in another room while you actually rest. If you need a human, pick the one who already knows and does not forward.

You are tired because a body thing happened. Curiosity is not a medical need. Let "I'm okay and I'm not discussing it" be the whole file. She can want Uncle's story. She cannot require yours.
