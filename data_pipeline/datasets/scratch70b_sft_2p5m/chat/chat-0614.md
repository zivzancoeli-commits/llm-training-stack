### User
My fob died in the parking lot. The dealer said the replacement is delayed, "maybe tomorrow." It is 9:20 p.m. My spare is in a drawer inside the apartment. The building fob is on the same ring, so I am outside the lobby too. My phone is at 18 percent. I don't want you to invent a locksmith price. I want the next hour: who I call, where I sit, how I do not spend the night on a planter.

### Assistant
You need a door, then a bed, then a fob story in daylight. The dealer is a tomorrow problem. Tonight is people who can open glass.

Call in this order, short battery: a neighbor who actually answers, a roommate, then the building emergency number on the keypad or sticker. Sentence: "I'm locked out, fob dead, spare is inside, can you buzz me in." Treat both building and car as closed until a human proves otherwise.

I will not invent a locksmith's fee. If you call one, ask the price before they roll and decide with their number, not mine. If you cannot pay it, sit in a public 24-hour place you already know while a human with a key moves.

Phone at 18: low-power mode, one text to two people. Screenshot the dealer delay for tomorrow. Tonight it does not open a door.

Are you still in the lot, and is there a lit lobby with a camera you can stand in?

### User
Lit lobby, locked. I can see the mailboxes. I texted my downstairs neighbor. No reply. Roommate is on a shift until 11. Dealer lot is a twenty-minute walk and they're closed. It's cold enough I want a jacket I also do not have.

### Assistant
Lobby glass is still better than a dark lot if it cuts wind. Stay in camera view if that feels safer than a side alley. Do not wander to "check another door" that looks propped unless you know that door. Propped doors are how nights get worse.

Text the roommate the truth: "Locked out, fob dead, I'll be in the front lobby or [diner name] until 11. Please come to the door." Then pick the diner if the lobby wind is mean and the diner is close. Walk while the phone still has a teen percent. Charge if they have a spare outlet and you have a cable. If you do not have a cable, buy time by sitting near staff, not on a planter. Staffed is the point.

If the neighbor never answers, that is done. Do not keep redialing a sleeper into a fight.

I will not tell you to break a window. I will not tell you a coat is worth a smashed pane. Cold is a reason to go public and lit, not to do carpentry.

Eleven is a real clock. You can last to 11 in a diner. You cannot last a pride contest with a planter.

### User
There's a guy in the lobby on the other side who I don't know. He's looking at me. I don't want to mime through glass and I don't want to look like I'm casing the mailboxes.

### Assistant
Step a little off the mailboxes. Phone to your ear or to your notes so you look like a locked-out resident, which you are. You do not have to mime a biography. If he opens the door, you can say, "I live in 2B, fob died, waiting on my roommate." If you do not want a stranger's door, you can shake your head and point at your phone and walk toward the diner. Both are allowed. I will not tell you he is safe. I will tell you glass plus a story plus a wait is normal, and so is leaving.

If your body says leave, leave. Diner, 11, roommate, charge. Dealer tomorrow. A spare that is not only the inside drawer is a weekend job. Tonight is heat and a human at 11.

Do not sleep in the car if the car is also fob-locked. Public and staffed beats a closed sedan.

Hour plan: diner or lobby, one text, 11, no planter. You get inside when a known person turns a lock. Until then you stay lit. Cold is real. Pride is not a jacket. Walk.
