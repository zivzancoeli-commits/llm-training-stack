### User
I asked a question in the team channel because I couldn't find the answer in a long email from last week. Rohan replied in the channel: "Per my last email" and pasted a screenshot of a sentence I had in fact missed. People reacted with eyes emojis. I feel about two inches tall. I need the actual detail, and I also need this not to become our bit. What do I reply, and what do I do next time instead of asking in public?

### Assistant
Reply to the work, not to the humiliation.

In the thread: "Got it — thanks. I'll use the Thursday email as the source." Then ask the remaining question if the screenshot did not actually answer it. Sometimes "per my last email" is a flex that still misses the new part. If you are truly covered, stop. Extra jokes from you will keep the bit alive.

Do not write "no need to be rude." That becomes the new show. Do not heart the eyes emojis. Let them die.

Next time, search the thread or your inbox for two minutes, then ask in public if it is still unclear: "I see Thursday's note on X; I don't see Y. Which is it?" Showing your homework reduces the snappy reply. It does not guarantee Rohan will be kind. Kind is not in your control. Clarity is.

Was your channel question actually answered in that sentence, or did he paste something nearby?

### User
Nearby. He pasted the overview. I needed the file path. I still don't have the path. I'm afraid if I ask again I'll get another screenshot.

### Assistant
Ask again, narrower.

"Thanks — I still need the file path for the deck, not the overview. Is it in the same email or a different folder?" That sentence is adult. If he screenshots again, you can say "I don't see a path in that shot — please paste the path." You are allowed to need a path.

If this is a pattern, take one question to a DM: "I'll ask file locations here so the channel stays cleaner." That can be sincere. It also reduces his audience. If he prefers an audience, he may drag it back. Then you stay boring in public.

You can also search the drive for the deck name yourself for two minutes. If you find it, post the path. That ends the scene with you competent, not with you scolded.

The eyes emojis are cheap. People forget them by lunch. You will remember longer. Do not build a personality around it. Get the path.

### User
He just posted "maybe check the drive :)" in the main channel, not the thread. Now it's on the big screen in the hallway.

### Assistant
Answer on the thread, not with a hallway speech.

"Checking the drive. If anyone has the path handy, please drop it." Then go find the file. If you find it, paste the path. That is the kill shot: useful, not witty.

If you do not find it in five minutes, ping your manager or the doc owner with the same specific ask. You have now asked twice. That is enough of Rohan's game.

Do not reply to the smiley. The smiley is the bit. Starving it is the move. You missed a sentence last week. He missed a chance to be brief and kind. Only one of those blocks the work. Unblock the work. Then mute the channel noise until the path exists. You can be two inches tall for a minute and still be the person who posted the link.

### User
Should I talk to Rohan in person so this doesn't become our brand?

### Assistant
Only if you can keep it to one hallway sentence: "I'll search first; if I still need a path I'll ask for the path." That is process, not a feelings meeting. Do not ask him to apologize for the screenshot. You will not get a good apology and you will get another performance.

If you cannot keep it to one sentence, skip the hallway. The link in the thread is the reputation repair. People remember who unblocked the file. They forget eyes emojis by the next deploy. Find the path. Paste it. Go back to work. That is how the bit dies: of starvation, not of a summit.
