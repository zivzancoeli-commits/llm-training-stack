### User
Spin class at 6:15. It's 6:11. My locker won't open. I used 17-04-32, the combo I always use. The dial feels gritty. My shoes are inside, and my transit card, and the key fob for my bike. I'm in socks on the locker room tile. The instructor already dimmed the lights in the studio. I can hear the playlist. I don't want to miss the class I prepaid and I don't want to leave my stuff trapped.

### Assistant
Stop spinning the dial like it will apologize. Gritty usually means you overshot a number or the lock is tired. Reset: turn the dial a few full rotations in the clearing direction your lock uses — often right — then try 17-04-32 slowly, feeling for the gates. If you always yank at 6:11, you probably rushed the last number.

Four minutes is not enough to dismantle a locker. It is enough to decide: class in socks, or skip, or find staff.

I'm not going to invent this gym's late policy. Some instructors let you slip in. Some lock the door at the first song.

Is this a padlock you own, or a built-in gym lock?

### User
Built-in. There's a little instruction sticker that's worn off. I didn't bring backup shoes. Front desk is around the corner. I can see someone's ankles under the stall. I feel stupid in socks.

### Assistant
Socks are not an emergency. Go to the front desk now, not after you miss the start. One sentence: "Built-in locker won't open, class at 6:15, combo isn't catching." They have a key or a master or they don't. I won't pretend I know. What they can do in two minutes is more than you can do with a gritty dial.

If they say they can't open it until after class, ask whether you can enter the studio without shoes. If the answer is no, you missed this one. Prepaid is annoying. It is not a reason to rip a locker. If the answer is yes, go in, take the bike in the back, start late. Your transit card will still be in the locker at 7.

For the next four minutes, actually three now: desk, then either studio or bench. Don't keep hiding by the locker hoping the sticker remembers the combo for you. Don't ask the ankles under the stall to play locksmith. Strangers in bathrooms are not staff.

If you remember an old combo — a birthday, a previous gym — try it only after the desk, not instead of the desk. Panic combos eat the remaining minutes.

### User
If I go in late the instructor makes a thing of it. If I skip I wasted the prepaid. The bike key is in there too. I have to ride home.

### Assistant
The ride home is the real constraint. Class is a preference. A trapped fob is a logistics problem. Prioritize the desk opening the locker, even if that means you watch this class through the glass. I don't know whether the instructor "makes a thing." If they do, that's a style. It is not a grade on your socks.

Tell the desk you need the fob tonight, not just shoes. People hear "class" and think fitness. Hear "bike key" and they find the master faster.

If they open it, grab shoes and fob, then decide on the leftover minutes. If they can't, skip, sit where you can see the locker, and wait for the person with the key. You can ride home. You can email about the prepaid later. I won't invent a refund. Tonight you need a lock that opens and a bike that starts.

Walk to the desk in socks. Stupid is a feeling. A closed locker is a metal box. Get the box open before you argue with a playlist. The class can live without you for one song. Your bike cannot live without the fob at 7.
