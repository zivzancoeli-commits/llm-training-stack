### User
Coworker asked me to "just capture the room" in a meeting they will run. After, they want my notes so they can present "a synthesis" to the director as theirs. I would be typing. They would be the smart one in a slide. I have done unpaid brain for them before. I don't want a fight in the calendar. I also don't want my bullets with their name. Meeting is this afternoon.

### Assistant
Capture the room is fine if you're the named note-taker. Synthesis with their name on your bullets is a different job.

Before the meeting: "I can take notes and share them with the group. I'm not going to send you a private digest for a solo presentation." Group notes make the thinking communal. Private digest is a ghostwrite.

If they still want a synthesis, they can write it from the shared notes like everyone else. You can even offer to sit as note-taker on the invite so the role is visible. Visible roles get less stolen.

I will not invent your company's credit norms. If your team already uses a shared doc, that's the path.

Have they already told the director you'll be "supporting," or is this a side ask?

### User
Side ask in a DM. Director thinks they own the topic. I know the topic better. That's why they want my notes. If I refuse they might just screenshot my laptop in the room.

### Assistant
Angle your laptop. Not a joke. Privacy screen if you have one. Notes go into a shared doc from the start, not a personal file they can hover over. Shared doc in the chat at minute one: "Notes here." Now a screenshot is of a public page, not a theft of a secret.

Refuse the private digest in the same DM so it exists. Short.

If they present your sentences as theirs, you can follow the director meeting with "adding the notes doc for reference" so the source sits in the thread. That's not a fight in the calendar. That's a link.

Knowing the topic better doesn't obligate you to donate a brain. You can still speak in the room. Speaking is harder to rebrand than silent bullets.

### User
What if I take notes and they still do the slide and I look petty if I care.

### Assistant
Caring about your name isn't petty. A scene is optional.

You already set the rule: shared notes, no private digest. If the slide happens, you send the doc, you speak next time, you stop doing extra capture for them. Petty would be a sarcastic Slack emoji. A link is not sarcastic.

Afternoon: shared doc, DM on record, you talk when you have the better fact, they can synthesize in public. Your bullets can live without their title page. That's a clean meeting, not a heist.

### User
Shared doc is in the chat. They still asked in a whisper for a "smart version" after. I said no. Director meeting is tomorrow. I think they'll reconstruct my bullets from memory and still present.

### Assistant
They can try. Your doc is in the thread with a timestamp. Tomorrow you can be in the room if you're invited, or you can send "notes doc from yesterday for reference" to the director thread if you're supposed to be in that loop. Don't send it as a gotcha. Send it as the artifact.

Whisper-after is the ghostwrite in analog. You already said no. Don't stay late making the smart version they were denied. Memory reconstruction will be thinner than your bullets. That's their problem.

You know the topic better. You can speak tomorrow if you're there. Speaking is hard to rebrand. Silent private notes were the theft vector. You closed it. Afternoon meeting: laptop angled, doc public, DM on record. That's enough spine for a calendar without a fight on camera.
