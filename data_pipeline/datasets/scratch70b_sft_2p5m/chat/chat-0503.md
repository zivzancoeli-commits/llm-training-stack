### User
I'm on a first date that is going fine. The server already dropped the check in the middle of the table like a test. I need to split it. I budgeted for my own dinner. I did not budget to audition for someone else's idea of chivalry. I have a history of freezing and then Venmoing later, which always feels worse. They haven't reached for the card yet. I need words that aren't a finance lecture.

### Assistant
Reach for it while you speak so your hands and your mouth agree. "Let's split this." If you want one extra clause, "I prefer to pay for mine." That's the lecture. There isn't a second page.

If they say "I got it," you can accept only if you actually want to. If you don't, you repeat, "Thanks — I'd still like to split." Repeating is not a fight. It's a preference landing.

Don't invent a story about your card rewards unless that's true and short. Don't suddenly become poor as a tactic. Don't suddenly become wealthy as a tactic. The bill is a bill.

I will not tell you what a fair date "should" cost or who "should" pay. I don't know your culture, their culture, or the menu. I know you budgeted for your plate. Honor that.

Ask the server, if needed, "Can we split?" Servers hear this all night. You are not inventing a new procedure.

Have you ordered similar amounts, or did one of you go much bigger?

### User
I got a pasta. They got a pasta and a cocktail and they suggested an appetizer we shared. I don't want to nickel-and-dime the appetizer. I also don't want to swallow the cocktail. They just joked "the universe will decide" about the check.

### Assistant
The universe will not decide. One of you will. You can smile at the joke without adopting it. "I'll take my pasta and half the appetizer. You take the drink." That's not nickel-and-diming. That's not swallowing a cocktail. If math in public feels ugly, a clean 50/50 is also fine if the difference is small to you. You already said the drink is the part you don't want. So don't 50/50 if that bothers you. Name the drink as theirs, once, without a receipt recitation.

If they look disappointed, let their face exist. You are not required to buy a better mood.

If the server can itemize, let them. If they can't, you pay the card and they send you their share before you leave, or you each put a card down. Don't do the later-Venmo if later-Venmo is the freeze you came here to avoid. Later is how resentment marinates.

"Universe" jokes are often a polite reach for the whole check. You can treat it as a reach: "I've got mine." Then you put your card on the tray. Physical card beats philosophy.

### User
I'm worried they'll think I'm not serious about them because I wouldn't let them pay.

### Assistant
A first date is information, not a mortgage. Someone who treats a split as a verdict on your seriousness is giving you information too. You can like them and still pay for pasta. If they need to fund the night to feel chosen, that's their story. You don't have to act in it.

If the rest of the date is fine, you keep talking after the cards come back. The bill is ten minutes, not the thesis of the relationship.

Right now: "Let's split — I'll take pasta and half the app, you take the drink." Card down. No lecture. No freeze, no later. You budgeted for your dinner. Eat the ending of it like a person who meant that. If the server asks "together or separate?" you can answer "separate" before the universe joke gets a second verse. Hands and mouth together. The date can survive a split check. A freeze-and-Venmo later is the version you already don't like. Don't pick it again.
