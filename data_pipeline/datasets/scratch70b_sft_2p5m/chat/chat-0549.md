### User
I buy coffee beans. Roommate drinks them. Not a sip — a pot, on days I'm not even home. They said "I thought we were coffee people." We are not a we on this. I have a cheap machine. They have a mug with my grounds in it right now. I need a script that isn't a TED talk about respect.

### Assistant
"Those are my beans. If you want coffee, buy a bag. You can use the machine." That's the script. Kitchen, now, while the mug exists.

Coffee people is a vibe. A bag with your receipt is property. You don't need a speech about respect. You need a closed loop: they replace this bag or they stop.

I will not invent what beans cost. "Buy your own" doesn't require a brand lecture. If they want to do a shared bag, that's a new agreement with money from both, not a backdated commune.

Don't lock the beans in your room unless talking fails twice. Start with talking. Locks are a later chapter and they make apartments feel like a sitcom.

Are they otherwise okay about food, or is this the same as the soap-and-milk pattern?

### User
They're okay-ish about labeled leftovers. Coffee wasn't labeled. It lived next to the machine, which I guess looked like a hotel setup. They looked confused that I'm annoyed. The pot is already brewed. I don't want it.

### Assistant
Hotel setup is on you a little, on them a little. Next to the machine is convenient. It is not a sign that says "unlimited."

You can still claim it. "Unlabeled isn't the same as shared. I'm not drinking that pot. Next bag is yours or we split a bag on purpose." Confused is useful. Confused people can still hear a rule.

If they offer the pot to you as a peace gift, you can decline. You didn't want that brew. Declining the pot is not wasting a peace offering. It's you not pretending.

Label the next bag with your name if you must. A piece of tape is not a TED talk. It's a speed bump for a sleepy roommate.

Shared-on-purpose looks like: you both put in for a bag, you both drink it, you both replace it. If they don't want that, they buy a cheaper bag and keep it on their shelf. Machine can stay communal. Beans don't have to.

### User
What if they say they'll "just use a little" from now on.

### Assistant
A little is how the bag dies without a moment you can point at. "Zero from my bag unless we split." Little is a no.

If they want a little, they want a shared bag with money. Until that exists, their mug gets their coffee.

Tonight: one kitchen paragraph, no respect seminar, pot they brewed is theirs to drink or dump, next beans assigned. You can be a coffee person with a private bag. That's allowed in a kitchen that isn't a lobby.

### User
I said zero from my bag. They bought a cheaper bag and put it next to mine. This morning they used mine anyway because theirs "wasn't open yet." The cheaper bag is still sealed like a prop.

### Assistant
Open is a one-second job. Sealed as a prop is the old commune.

"Yours is right there. Mine stays mine. Open yours." If you need a physical speed bump, your bag goes on your shelf, not next to the machine. Hotel setup trained them. You can untrain the location without a speech about respect.

If they "forget" again, you don't pour them a mug to keep peace. Peace that drinks your beans is just a tab. Cheaper bag exists. They can break the seal. Machine stays shared. Your bag does not. That's the same policy with a photo of the unopened bag if you need to point at the joke. Tonight you already said zero. Tomorrow you enforce location. Coffee people can mean two bags.
