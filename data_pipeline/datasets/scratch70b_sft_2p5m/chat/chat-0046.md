### User
Flight's delayed. Board says two hours, which I don't believe. My phone is at twelve percent. The guy in the next seat has the only open outlet and a charger in it, and he's watching a show. I have a cable but no block. I feel like if I ask wrong I'll start a fight in Gate B. I also don't want a pep talk about travel being an adventure.

### Assistant
No adventure. It's a delay and a battery.

Two options, then a recommendation. I'm not going to invent what the airline owes you, and I won't pretend I know when you'll push back. The phone needs to survive boarding plus the mess after.

Option A: ask, timed and specific. "If I plug in for twenty minutes, I'll unplug and you can have it back. I've got my own cable." You're borrowing the block, not his evening.

Option B: walk. Outlets hide by windows, unused gates, pillars, charging islands. Hunt for five minutes. If you find nothing, do A, or switch to airplane mode and accept a dumb rectangle.

I'd hunt five minutes first, then ask. People get snippy when you treat their outlet as communal furniture. A short hunt costs almost nothing. If the hunt finds a free wall socket with no block in it, that's still a miss unless you can borrow a block without hovering. Don't stand over someone's bag "waiting your turn" like the outlet is a bus stop. Either ask once or walk on.

### User
I walked. There's a charging island but every brick is taken and people are sitting on the floor like it's a camp. The guy is still there. Twelve percent is now ten. I hate asking.

### Assistant
Hate it and still ask, or hate it and go airplane mode. Resentment at ten percent does not charge the phone.

If you ask, keep it dry. No joke, no delay story. He can see the board. "Mind if I use the block for twenty minutes? I'll set a timer." If he says no, you say okay and mean it. Airplane mode, write down gate-change info, stop opening maps.

If he says yes, set the timer where he can see it. Don't start a show on his goodwill. I won't guess the percent you need. "Enough to show a boarding pass and call a ride" is the job, not "full."

I'd still recommend asking once. Ten percent plus a maybe-two-hour delay is a bad combo. Conservation is the backup. A sentence is not a fight. A follow-up argument is.

### User
He said I could plug in if I don't unplug his phone. So both of us on a split, I guess. Is that a trick? Also the delay just jumped again.

### Assistant
It's not a trick. It's a two-port block or a strip, or some combo I can't see. Plug into the spare mouth if there is one. If there isn't: "I don't want to unplug you. I'll go conserve." Don't yank his phone. Don't grab the faster port if you don't know which is which.

The delay jumping is why you wanted charge. Still no pep talk: delays stack, batteries don't. Get what you can in twenty minutes, then airplane mode so you're not burning it on refresh-the-board.

If he talks, be civil without becoming seatmates for life. If he doesn't, silence is fine. You asked for a charger, not a friend. If the delay jumps again while you're plugged in, don't use that as an excuse to ignore the timer. The extra wait is the airline's mess. His block is still his. When you unplug, you can screenshot the new gate if you have the percent; if you don't, write the gate on a napkin. Analog still works when batteries don't.

### User
Plugged into the spare. Timer on. I'll kill the refresh habit. If he needs it back, he gets it back. No adventure narrative.

### Assistant
You hunted, you asked, you got a spare port. Keep the timer honest. When it rings, you can ask "okay if I stay until fifty" *once*. If he hesitates, unplug. Leaving on time is how you don't become the villain of Gate B.
