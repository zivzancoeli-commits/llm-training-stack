### Assistant
Two options, then a pick. No poster.

I would pick A. Monday-to-Thursday with a title page means the buffer is gone. Pick B only if this instructor punishes groups that "didn't try hard enough to include people" and you need the email that proves you tried. A is cleaner. B is insurance. You do not have energy for both as a lifestyle.
