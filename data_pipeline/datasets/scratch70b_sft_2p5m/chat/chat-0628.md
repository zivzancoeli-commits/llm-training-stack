### User
The roof deck has a party. Subwoofer. It is 12:25. My ceiling is the drum. They said the invite was "until 11, neighbor-friendly." I can hear a laugh track of people who do not live under the speaker. I don't want a cop night. I want the bass to die. How do I do this without a building war or a fake ordinance?

### Assistant
They already wrote until 11. It is 12:25. You are not inventing a vibe. You are collecting a promise.

I will not invent a noise ordinance. I will not invent a towing rule for speakers. You can still go up, or text the host if you have them, with: "It's 12:25, invite said 11, the sub is in my ceiling. Please kill the bass or end it." Kill the bass is smaller than end the party. Sometimes they can turn a knob. Ask for the knob first.

If you go up, shoes, one sentence, do not accept a beer as a silencer. Beer is how you become a guest at your own insomnia. If they point at "almost done," you can say, "Bass off now, done soon is not a time." Then you leave the roof. Do not stay to debate playlists.

If there is a front desk or a night person, they can carry the same sentence. That is not automatically cops. It is staff.

Do you know the host, or is this a random building roof?

### User
I know Maya from 4B. She posted the invite. I liked it at 6 p.m. like an idiot. Desk is a phone tree. I can hear them moving chairs, so it might be ending, but the sub is still on. I don't want to be the neighbor who liked then snitched.

### Assistant
Liking at 6 was until-11 you. 12:25 is a different you. You are allowed to change when the sub does not. Text Maya, not a building-wide thread: "Hope it's fun. It's after 12 and the sub is in my ceiling. Can you kill the bass? Invite said 11." Hope-it's-fun is the leftover like. Kill the bass is the ask. That is not a snitch. That is the host you know.

If chairs are moving, they may be close. A sub can still drone while people pack. The knob still matters. If she says five minutes, set a timer. If the timer hits and the ceiling still thumps, desk phone tree or a second text: "Still going." Two contacts, not a raid.

Do not bang a broom unless that is a language your building already uses and you know it works. Brooms start wars. Texts name a friend.

Idiot is a strong word for a like. Retract it with a time.

### User
She replied "come up, don't be a grandpa." The sub just did a drop. I am in socks.

### Assistant
Do not go up in socks to prove you are not a grandpa. Grandpa is a hook. Shoes if you go. Better: "Not coming up. Please kill the sub. I need sleep." If you do go, one sentence at the door of the roof, not a tour of cool. Then down.

If she will not kill it, you try the desk tree. I will not tell you to call a non-emergency line. I do not know your city. A rooftop with a named host is often a building problem first. Staff, then bed, then a morning note if you want a paper trail: time, sub, invite said 11. Morning-you can decide if it needs more. Night-you needs bass off.

Neighbor-friendly was their phrase. Use it: "You said neighbor-friendly. The sub isn't." Then mute the thread. Put plugs in. Lie down on the part of the bed that thumps less if that exists.

You can like a person at 6 and still want a ceiling at 12:30. That is not a war. That is a knob. Ask for the knob. Stay off the dance floor. Let the laugh track finish without you as the extra. Bass dies or you document. Either way you do not have to be fun on a roof in socks. Fun already had until 11. You are after. After gets quiet. Text that. Then wait for the drop to not be your pulse.
