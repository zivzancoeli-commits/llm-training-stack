### User
I ordered an iced oat latte, extra hot, which I now realize is a contradiction I said out loud. They handed me a lukewarm cup that tastes like perfume. I think they used almond. I said oat. There's a line. I have a 8:10 train. It's 7:52. I have never sent a drink back. I don't want to sip perfume to the office and I don't want to be a bit.

### Assistant
Extra hot iced was always going to be a confused ticket. That's on the order. Almond versus oat is on the cup if you said oat. You can still ask. A remake is not a bit. A bit is a speech about your journey with milk.

Don't drink more to "make sure." You already know. Walk back to the handoff counter, not the line's front. One sentence: "I asked for oat. This tastes like almond. Could you remake it?" Put the cup on the counter. Don't pour it out in the trash first; they may want to see it. Don't accuse a person of ruining your morning.

I won't invent cafe policy. Some places remake, some point at the mobile ticket. You have 18 minutes. That's enough for a remake or enough to dump it and board with water.

Did you order at the register or on the app, and does the cup sleeve say the milk?

### User
Register. Sleeve says "i oat" in marker, smudged. Could be "i alm." I said oat twice because the first time they said "sorry?" Train is the 8:10 from the station two blocks away. If I miss it I wait 22 minutes and I'm late to standup.

### Assistant
Smudged sleeve is your exhibit. You said oat twice. That's plenty. Don't add a third autobiography.

For the next ten minutes: remake request, then start walking even if the drink isn't perfect. A lukewarm perfume cup is not worth a missed 8:10. If they remake fast, great. If they look at the line like you're a problem, you can say "if it's going to be a minute I'll take a plain oat iced and go." Simpler drink, less perfume, faster.

Don't wait for extra hot on ice. That order was a muddle. Plain iced oat latte is a sentence a sleepy cafe can hit.

If they refuse, leave the cup, buy nothing else, walk. I won't guess whether they'll refund. You can sort money later. Standup cares about 8:10 more than a dollar.

Two blocks at 7:52 is doable if you don't stay to supervise the steam wand. Ask, accept the next cup or none, move your feet.

### User
What if they remake it and it's still wrong and I have to smile because they watched me? I freeze when people watch me taste.

### Assistant
You don't have to taste as theater. Take the cup, step aside, sniff. If it's still almond, you do not owe a second performance. You can leave it on the condiment bar and go. One remake is the ask. Two remakes is how you miss the train. I know that's unsatisfying. 8:10 doesn't care.

If it's oat and you still hate the perfume note, that might be the syrup or the beans. You don't have to finish it. Toss it on the platform if you need your hands. You're allowed to have wasted a cup and still be on time.

Smile is optional. "Thanks" is enough. Freeze is your nervous system, not a verdict on whether you were allowed to speak. You already spoke. That's the first time done.

Walk at 7:56 whether the second cup exists or not. Standup at the other end is the real appointment. The cafe will still be there tomorrow with a shorter order: iced oat latte, no extra hot, no novel. Tonight's lesson is the ticket, not your character. Get to the station. You can be a person who asked once and then caught a train. That's the win, not a perfect milk.
