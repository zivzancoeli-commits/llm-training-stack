### User
I have bought four umbrellas since March. They leave with Sasha "just to the train" and never come back. Today's rain started and the hook by the door is empty again. I asked once in May and they said "oh yeah I'll grab it." They did not. I don't want a laminated sign. I want my next umbrella to live here. How do I do this without a speech about respect?

### Assistant
Stop buying communal rain gear. The hook is a lending library Sasha treats as a gift shop. Change the system, then say one sentence. Four umbrellas is a pattern, not a mix-up.

Buy one if you need it tomorrow, and put it in your room, not on the hook. The hook is the disappearance machine. Door hook is for things you can lose. Bedroom is for things you need. If you have a work bag that already holds a compact, load that tonight so morning-you is not bargaining with an empty peg.

Say: "I need you to stop taking my umbrellas. The last ones didn't come back. Use your own or the dollar one, not mine." If they say they will grab it, give a date: "Bring the current one back by Friday or replace it." I will not invent a price. Replace means an umbrella that opens, not a vibe.

Do not start with respect. Start with the object. Four missing canopies is the evidence.

Is there one currently in their bag, or is this a ghost pile?

### User
I saw a black one that looks like mine in their bike basket yesterday. They said it was "probably theirs." We both buy black. I feel insane doing a lineup.

### Assistant
Skip the lineup. Black umbrellas all look like cousins. From now on yours is not black on a shared hook. Pick a color so loud it looks like a toy, or put a zip tie on the handle in a color they do not use. Marking is dull and it ends the probably-theirs talk.

For the basket: "If that's the one from the hook, I need it back tonight. I'm not doing a maybe." If they refuse, you drop the archaeology. You protect the next one. Hunting ghosts is how you stay mad and still get wet.

Friday deadline still works for a replacement if nothing comes back. If they joke, do not joke. Jokes are how umbrellas emigrate.

You can also put a hook inside your closet. Extra steps on a rainy morning beat a fourth funeral.

### User
They'll say I'm being territorial about a ten dollar thing. I can hear it. Also I don't want them stranded in rain. I'm not a villain.

### Assistant
Territorial is a word people use when they liked the old library. You can say, "You can get stranded with your own plan. I'm keeping one dry." Kindness is not an infinite canopy. If you want a house spare, buy one ugly spare, label it HOUSE, and accept that HOUSE may vanish. Your personal one stays in the room. Two-tier is how you stop being a villain and a sucker at once.

I will not price the ten dollars as a moral law. Cheap things still take four replacement trips. Your time in the drugstore is the real fee.

If they come home wet and pointed, you can still lend a HOUSE spare for one walk and take it back at the door. Lend with your hand still on the handle is different from the hook disappearing overnight. Door-to-door loan, not an open tab.

Tonight: mark or move the next umbrella, one sentence to Sasha, no laminated sign, no lineup. Say it while you are both in the hallway so it is attached to the empty hook, not to a group chat essay. If the basket one appears on the hook, take it to your room. Rain is coming again. Let theirs be theirs. Let yours survive a Tuesday.
