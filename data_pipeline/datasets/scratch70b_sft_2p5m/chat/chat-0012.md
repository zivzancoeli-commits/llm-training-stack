title: Talk through a barista getting a complicated order wrong twice

### User
I asked for a medium oat cortado, extra shot, no foam art, cup not mug. First one was dairy and a mug. I said so. Second one is oat but it's a latte in a paper cup with a mountain of foam and I think only one shot. The line is long. The barista looks wrecked. Last year I would have smiled and drunk it and hated my afternoon. I do not want a third experiment and I also don't want to write them a novel. How do I say no?

### Assistant
You say no to this cup. You do not invent a dairy allergy you do not have, a train you are not catching, or a boss who will fire you for foam. Those stories make you feel polite and they teach the bar that the order was optional.

Short line: "This still isn't the cortado. I don't want this one. I need the medium oat cortado, extra shot, in a cup." Then you stop. You can add "thank you" if your mouth needs a landing, but you do not add a biography.

They look wrecked. That is real. It does not convert the drink into yours. You can be a person who notices they are slammed and still refuse the wrong cup. Both can sit in the same sentence if you need them to: "I know it's slammed. I still can't take this."

Do not start a third custom. If they offer to remake it, yes, remake the original. If they offer a random pastry to make you go away, you can decline the pastry too. Same rule: no fake reason. "No thanks, just the drink I ordered."

Are you paying by card on an app tab, or is this a register order they can void?

### User
App tab. The second drink is already marked complete. I feel like I'm going to get the "we'll remake it" look and then a third wrong thing while people stare.

### Assistant
App tabs love the word complete. Complete is not the same as correct. You still stand at the handoff and you still say the sentence. If they need to tap something on their screen, that is their problem to solve. You do not apologize to the line. The line is not the customer of your order.

If a remake starts, you can reduce failure points without shrinking yourself: "Cortado, oat, extra shot, paper cup, no extra foam." Count them on your fingers if you have to, not as a joke, as a checklist they can hear. Then you step aside so the line can move, but you stay where they can find you. Hiding at a table is how last-year-you ended up drinking the mountain of foam.

If the third cup is wrong, you do not invent a meeting. You say "I'll cancel the drink" or "please refund the app order." I will not quote a price or a corporate policy. I do not know their refund rules. You can ask "can you refund this on the app?" If they say no, you still do not have to drink it. Leaving a wrong drink on the counter is allowed. Pouring it into yourself to avoid waste is how you teach your afternoon to be bitter.

You are not required to soothe their wrecked face. A nod is enough human. A speech about labor conditions, while true in the abstract, is not your job in this line. Say no, stay specific, take the correct cup or take the refund path they offer.

Last-year-you smiled. This-year-you can be brief. Brief is kinder to a busy bar than a novel and kinder to you than a latte you did not want. No excuse. No third flavor of compromise. The order was already the compromise with a complicated drink. Hold it.

If your hands shake, that is leftover niceness, not evidence you are wrong. Put the bad cup on the counter, say the line once, and wait. You paid for a cortado. You are allowed to not swallow a latte twice.
