### User
I woke up at 5:50 with a feverish chill and a throat that feels like sandpaper. I have a 9 a.m. standup and a 10:30 with a vendor. I never call in. I once went to work with a migraine and it became a personality. I need to tell my manager. I keep drafting "maybe I can still do the vendor call on camera off." I live alone. There's no one to make tea except me. It's 6:12. I haven't emailed.

### Assistant
If you have a chill and sandpaper, you stay home. Camera-off vendor is still work. First time calling in while actually sick should not be a negotiation with your migraine history.

I'm not a clinician. I will not diagnose flu, strep, or anything else. I will not tell you a temperature number I don't have. I will say: feverish plus throat at 5:50 is enough to cancel a 9 a.m. performance.

Draft is stalling. Send. Short: "I'm sick today — chills, sore throat. I need to take sick time. Please have someone cover standup and reschedule the 10:30 vendor. I'll be offline to rest." Offline is the point. Camera-off is a leak.

Do you have a sick-day process — Slack, email, a form — or is it just the manager's inbox?

### User
Email plus a Slack to the team. There's a form nobody uses. Vendor is a person I've already postponed once in March. I feel like I'll look flaky. Tea kettle is on. I haven't taken anything because I can't remember if I have acetaminophen or only allergy pills.

### Assistant
Don't take random pills in the dark. If you can't tell the bottles apart, leave them until you can read labels in the kitchen light. I'm not going to dose you. Water is safe. Tea is fine if you can swallow.

March postpone is a fact. Today is a different fact. You can tell the vendor, or ask your manager to: "Need to move 10:30, I'm out sick." One line. Flaky is a story you tell about a migraine you worked through. Resting is how you don't make a sequel.

For the next ten minutes: send the manager email, Slack the team that you're out, put the laptop on a shelf you can't reach from bed. If you keep it on the nightstand you'll "just check." Checking is the vendor call in a weaker shirt.

Form nobody uses can wait unless your job truly requires it. If it does, do it in two minutes, not as a project.

Cancel 9 and 10:30 like they are rooms you will not enter. Because you won't.

### User
What if I feel slightly better at 8:40 and I undo it? That's my pattern. Also I feel guilty about the vendor's calendar.

### Assistant
If you feel slightly better at 8:40, that's still not a standup. Slightly is how people infect a room and then collapse at 2. You already sent sick. Don't unsend. I'm not your doctor; if you worsen, you use whatever care path you already know. If you improve tomorrow, that's tomorrow.

Vendor's calendar can move. Yours cannot invent health. Guilt can sit in a chair in the other room. You don't have to serve it tea.

Stay off camera. Sleep in the leftover dark. Set one alarm for water at noon if you're a person who sleeps too hard, not eight alarms that pull you into Slack.

You went to work with a migraine once. That became a personality because nobody stopped you, including you. Today you stop. 6:12 email. Kettle. Labels in the light if you need them. No vendor heroics. First time can be uneventful on purpose. Uneventful is the skill. Send, then put the phone face down. The 9 a.m. can happen without your sandpaper voice. That's allowed. That's the job today: not doing the job. Take it. If guilt knocks at 8:40, let it knock. You already sent the mail. Stay in bed.
