### User
Break-room honesty. I ordered a complicated drink because I am tired and I wanted the usual. They got it wrong, I said something, they remade it, still wrong — oat instead of whole, and the extra shot is missing. They are starting a third cup. The line is staring. I do not have time to watch a third experiment and I do not want a refund speech. I also do not want to invent a meeting I do not have just to leave. How do I say no to the third attempt without being a villain and without a fake excuse.

### Assistant
You say no. You do not invent a meeting. Tired-coworker version, flat: I am going to skip the third one. This still is not the drink. I will take water, or I will take nothing, and I am going to go.

That is the whole sentence. You do not owe a story about your calendar. "I do not have time" is true if it is true. If you do have time and you are just done, say I am done. Done is a reason. Fake meetings are how you teach yourself to lie about small things.

If they have already started pouring, you can still say stop. Milk in a pitcher is not a moral debt. They can use it for the next oat drink.

Do not narrate their incompetence to the line. Lower voice, counter only. If a manager slides in, same no. I will not invent store policy. If you already paid, ask them to reverse it or not charge the remake. If you have not paid, you walk.

Was this a mobile order with the modifiers in the app, or a spoken order at the register?

### User
Spoken. I said whole milk, extra shot, no whip, extra hot. First cup was oat and whip. Second was whole, still whip, no extra shot, not hot. I keep feeling rude because they are trying. I also do not want the "can I get you a plain latte instead" upsell while I am holding two wrong cups.

### Assistant
Trying is not the same as getting it. You can respect effort and still refuse a third. Rude would be calling them names. A no is not a name.

Set the two cups on the counter. Say these two are not what I ordered; I do not want a third. If they ask what to do with them, that is their problem. You do not have to drink mistakes to be polite.

"Plain latte instead" is a new order. You are allowed to say no. No thanks. I am not ordering something else. Do not let them reset you into a simpler customer so they can feel successful. That is the upsell dressed as help.

If your mouth wants to soften it with I have a standup in four minutes, delete that. Softening with a clock you do not have is the same lie in a nicer shirt.

Take your card, confirm you are not being charged for two experiments plus a third, and go. You do not have to stay and regulate their feelings. You are on a break, not in HR.

### User
What if they insist the third is already made and they put it in my hand. And what if my coworker behind me in line says just take it so we can move.

### Assistant
If it is in your hand, you can set it down. Your hand is not a contract. Say I do not want this. Walk. If they follow you with the cup, you still do not have to drink it.

Coworker in line wants speed, not your dignity. Say I am not taking a third wrong drink, without turning around for a debate. They can order when you step aside. Stepping aside is how you stop blocking the line. Taking a drink you will dump in the trash is not kindness. It is waste plus sugar you did not want.

If you feel the villain feeling, name it privately: I said no. That is the job. You needed whole, extra shot, no whip, extra hot. You did not get it. Two tries. Stop.

Go back to the break room with tap water if you have to. Caffeine can wait an hour. A third experimental cup is how you stay stuck at the register performing patience you do not have. No. No extra story. Leave.
