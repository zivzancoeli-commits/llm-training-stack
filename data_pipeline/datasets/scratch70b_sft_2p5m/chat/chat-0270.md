### User
Elevator is out. Sign on the door says "tech notified" with no time. I have a stroller, a 10-month-old, groceries in the under-basket, and we're on the sidewalk. We live on the fourth floor. I can carry the baby. I cannot levitate the stroller and the milk. It's 5:50 p.m. Partner is on a bus 25 minutes away. Building front door is a keypad. Nobody is in the lobby.

### Assistant
Don't start the stairs with the loaded stroller as a dare. Fourth floor plus milk is how people twist an ankle and then have two problems.

Park the stroller where it isn't blocking the entrance. Take the baby and the milk first if you can do one trip with a hip carry and a bag. Leave the stroller locked or in sight. I don't know your lobby. If it's public sidewalk, bringing the stroller inside the vestibule, folded if you can, is better than the curb.

I'm not going to invent how long "tech notified" means. Call the number on the sign if there is one. Text the partner: "Elevator down, fourth, come to stairs when you get here."

Can you fold that stroller with one hand, or is it the kind that needs a wrestling match?

### User
It folds if I kneel. There's a building group chat. Last outage was two hours. Baby is hungry. Groceries include ice cream. I have a carrier in the hall closet on the fourth floor, which does not help me on the sidewalk.

### Assistant
Ice cream is a loss leader. Let it be soup. Milk and baby food matter more. Hungry baby on a sidewalk at 5:50 is the clock, not the frozen aisle.

For the next ten minutes: fold the stroller, get into the vestibule with keypad, carry baby up, park baby in the crib or on the floor with a toy if that's safe for a minute, then decide on a second trip. If you cannot leave a 10-month-old, you wait for the partner's 25 minutes in the vestibule or you ask the group chat: "Need a hand from sidewalk to 4, elevator out." One ask. Specific. Not a rant about tech.

Don't climb with baby in one arm and unfolded stroller bumping the rail. That's a cartoon injury.

If someone answers the chat, they carry stroller, you carry baby. If nobody answers, partner gets the stroller when they arrive. You go up with child and the perishables that aren't ice cream. Ice cream can die. That's allowed.

Carrier in the closet: once you're up, put the baby in it for trip two if you still need hands. Not before you have it.

### User
I feel like other parents would just do the stairs in one heroic trip. The group chat might think I'm dramatic.

### Assistant
Other parents are not on this sidewalk with your knees. Heroic trips are how people drop groceries and worse. Dramatic is a word people use about help. Help is a stroller on four flights.

Post the ask. If they think you're dramatic, they can scroll. If they come, you get a stroller. You don't have to perform capability for a group thread.

Wait in the vestibule with the baby if the climb with bags feels shaky. 25 minutes with a snack if you have one in the diaper bag. Partner plus two adults is a normal way to do a fourth floor. Elevators exist because stairs with wheels are a bad design.

Leave the ice cream. Fold. Keypad. Baby up. Chat. Partner. You don't need to levitate. You need two trips or two people. 5:50 is rush hour, not a test of your parent brand. Get the child inside. The stroller can be a second act. The sign can stay unhelpful. You already noticed. That's enough noticing. Move the easy weight first. That's the baby. Then the milk. Then the wheels. In that order.
