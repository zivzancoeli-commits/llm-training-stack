### User
I reserved the building freight elevator for 9–11 a.m. I paid the fee they asked. It is 9:40. The elevator is stuck on 12 with someone else's sofa. The mover's truck is in a loading zone with a timer. Super isn't answering. I have two people on hourly moving help standing in the lobby. I don't know if I should start using the passenger elevator, wait, or call the number on the reservation email. The sofa on 12 is not moving.

### Assistant
Call the reservation number now, then the super again, then the management line if those are different. Parallel is allowed. You already paid for a window. A sofa on 12 is their traffic problem as well as yours.

Do not start a full move in the passenger elevator unless the building has told you that is allowed. You can ask, on the same call: "Passenger elevator — yes or no for empty boxes?" Empty boxes are different from a dresser. A dresser in a passenger car is how you get a second complaint and a stop.

Tell your movers the clock: paid window, truck timer, you are calling. Idle hourly people should not be guessing. If they can stage in the lobby without blocking, stage. If they cannot, one of them can wait with the truck.

I will not invent your building's elevator rules or refund policy. Ask: "The reserved car is blocked. What is the backup, and what happens to the fee if we miss the window?" Write the answer.

Is the truck able to circle, or will it get ticketed if it leaves the zone?

### User
If it leaves, we lose the zone. There's no other spot. Movers said they'll start carrying small boxes up the stairs "to do something." That's four floors. Super's voicemail is full.

### Assistant
Stairs for a few small boxes can be a pressure valve if backs are willing. It is not the sofa plan. Do not let "do something" become a dresser on a landing.

Keep one person with the truck. Keep calling. If there is a front desk or a porter, they exist for this. Use them. "Freight reserved 9–11, blocked on 12, I need it cleared or an extension." Short.

If you can knock on 12 without being a menace, you can, once: "We have the freight reserved — any idea when this load is done?" They may not be the ones who jammed it. They may be. Information is still information.

Photo the elevator indicator at 9:40, 10:00. Timestamped photos help a fee argument later. They do not move a sofa now. Do them anyway while you wait.

If management offers a later window today, take it if the truck can legally exist. If they offer tomorrow, that is a different mess with the movers' hours. Ask the movers what a delay costs before you agree. I will not guess their hourly.

### User
Someone on 12 yelled "five more minutes" twenty minutes ago. The truck got a warning on the windshield. Not a ticket yet.

### Assistant
Five more minutes already failed. Treat it as unknown.

Ask the movers if they can sit in the truck with hazards if that is legal where you are — I will not pretend I know. If a warning is on the glass, one of you talks to whoever put it there, with the reservation email on your phone: "We paid for the freight; it's blocked; we're trying." Sometimes that buys a loop. Sometimes it doesn't.

If the freight opens, you go immediately, biggest items first. Reorder the load in your head now so you don't waste the car on lampshades.

If it does not open by the end of your paid window, you get the backup in writing — even a text. Fee, extra hour, tomorrow. You already paid once. Do not silently pay in tickets and overtime without asking.

You are not a bad planner. A sofa on 12 is not in your spreadsheet. Call, photo, truck person, small boxes only if backs agree. When the door opens, move like the clock is real, because it is. The reservation was the plan. Right now the plan is getting a working door and keeping the truck from becoming the next problem.
