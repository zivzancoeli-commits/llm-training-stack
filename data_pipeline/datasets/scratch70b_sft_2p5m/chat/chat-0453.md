### User
Chris wants me to come to a "wellness preview" on Thursday. The slides they forwarded have circles of people and a lot of the word residual. They said it's not a pyramid, it's a community. They want me to buy a starter kit so I "understand the products." I like Chris. I don't want the kit. I don't want to recruit my cousins. Thursday is my only night off. How do I decline without a TED talk about MLMs and without losing the friend if that's still possible?

### Assistant
You skip Thursday and you skip the kit. You can still like Chris.

"I'm not going to the preview and I'm not buying a kit. I don't want to sell stuff to people I know. Let's do something that's actually us another night." That's a no plus an alternate that isn't their funnel. If you don't want another night, skip the offer. Don't fake a rain check you'll resent.

Don't debate whether the diagram is a pyramid. They already told you it isn't. You don't need to win the geometry. "It's not for me" is enough. If they say you're closed-minded, you can say, "Yeah, I'm closed to this." Then stop.

I will not invent what the products cost, whether the company is "legal," or a medical claim about the powders. I won't tell you the powders work or don't. You don't want to sell. That's the decision.

Did they already put your name on a guest list, or is this still a text?

### User
Text. They said they "saved me a seat." There's a Zoom link too if I "can't do in person." They used a crying emoji. I always fold at crying emojis. Last year I bought a candle kit I never opened.

### Assistant
Don't attend the Zoom as a compromise. That's still a preview. "I'm not doing Thursday in person or on video. Don't save the seat. I'm out on the kit too." If the emoji arrives again, you can send a heart and the same no. You don't have to write a paragraph that teaches them about recruiting.

If they ask why, one line: "I don't want to sell to friends." If they say you wouldn't have to sell, only buy, that's still a kit. "I'm not buying." Two nouns, no essay.

Candle kit last year is useful memory. You don't need a new box in the closet to prove loyalty. Loyalty can be a pizza on Saturday that isn't a downline.

If they go quiet, wait. If they only talk to you in residual now, that's information. You can still send a normal meme next week. You're not in a war. You're not in a kit.

### User
They asked me to "just watch the first twenty minutes and decide." That's the hook, right?

### Assistant
It's the hook. Twenty minutes is how kits happen. "I already decided. I'm not watching." You don't have to sample a pitch to have a valid no. Curiosity is how they wrote the script.

Thursday: your night off stays off. If you feel guilty, put the guilt in a walk, not in a Zoom.

If they send a "last chance" photo of the room setup, you don't have to reply with an essay. One sticker, or nothing. The preview will happen without you. That's the point of a no.

If they ask you to "just tell two friends," that's recruiting with extra steps. "I'm not going to pass this along." Then you stop. Your cousins are not a list.

Thursday: your night off stays off. If you feel guilty, put the guilt in a walk, not in a Zoom. Next text: no seat, no Zoom, no kit, optional pizza later if you mean it. You don't need a lecture on pyramids. You need a night that isn't a preview. Chris can be your friend and also be wrong about your cousins. You already know. Send it before the emoji gets a second shift. The candle kit can stay unopened in the closet as a reminder, not as a reason to buy a second box.
