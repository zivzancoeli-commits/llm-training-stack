### User
Devon wants me to "be on their side" about a person named Rafi I have never met. Rafi is "a snake" who "stole" a group chat vibe. There's a 40-message voice-note. Devon asked me to leave Rafi off a birthday invite and to "not be friendly if they show." I don't even know what Rafi looks like. I don't want to lose Devon. I also don't want to join a hate I can't check. We're on a call. They're waiting. Help.

### Assistant
You can be Devon's friend without adopting an enemy.

"I'm with you that you're upset. I'm not going to hate someone I haven't met, and I'm not going to promise a cold shoulder at a party. I can listen. I won't run a campaign." That's the split. If they want a yes-man, they'll flinch. Let them.

Don't listen to 40 minutes of voice notes as a loyalty test. "I can't do a 40-message briefing. If you want to vent for ten minutes, I can." Then you set a timer in your head. Last year's you would have put in the hours and still not known Rafi.

I won't tell you Rafi is innocent or guilty. I have no facts. Neither do you, except Devon. That's allowed as Devon's story. It isn't a court.

On the invite: if it's your party, you decide the list. If it's Devon's, they can leave Rafi off without you performing disgust. If it's a shared friend group, you don't have to be the bouncer.

Are you on the call right now, and is the birthday yours?

### User
Call right now. Birthday is Devon's. I was going to help with balloons. They want me to text two people to "warn them about Rafi." That's the next ask, I can feel it.

### Assistant
Don't send the warning texts. That's a campaign.

"I'll help with balloons. I won't text people a warning about someone I don't know. If you don't want Rafi there, that's your invite." If Devon says you're not a real friend, you can say, "I can be a friend and not a messenger." Then you go back to balloons or you get off the call if it's only the hate now.

If they keep pushing the voice notes, "I believe you're hurt. I'm still not joining a hate." You don't need to litigate the group chat vibe. Vibe theft is not a charge you can investigate tonight.

If Rafi shows anyway, you can be polite and boring. You don't have to be warm. You don't have to spit. Neutral is allowed when you have no data.

Get off the call if you need: "I have to go. I'm not sending those texts. Talk tomorrow about balloons." That's an exit that still offers the help you actually meant.

### User
They said "so you're choosing Rafi." I haven't chosen anyone. The silence is loud. What do I say in the next sentence so this doesn't become a breakup over a stranger?

### Assistant
"I'm not choosing Rafi. I haven't met them. I'm choosing not to hate on command. I'm still coming to your birthday with balloons if you want me there." Then stop. Don't add a paragraph. If they hang up, you can text the balloon sentence once. You don't chase a verdict.

You can like Devon and still not rent your mouth. Next minute: that sentence, then balloons as a real yes or a real no. You don't owe a stranger hate. You don't owe Devon a campaign. You owe a clear line. Say it. The snake story can stay theirs. You stay a person who hasn't met Rafi. That's not a breakup unless they need a soldier. If they do, you'll find out. That's still better than a lie about a face you've never seen.

If the birthday happens and Rafi is there, you can still hold balloons and be boring. You don't have to perform a reunion or a snub. Neutral is a side that isn't a campaign. You already chose that. Keep choosing it when the room wants a show.
