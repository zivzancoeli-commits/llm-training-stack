### User
My birthday is Saturday. I have about enough money for a cheap pizza and not enough for a restaurant plus gifts plus that photo-booth place my group chat keeps sending. I'm fourteen. I don't want a pity party and I don't want to cancel and sit in my room like the day is radioactive. Two friends can come. One more might. My parent said they can drive people, not pay for a big thing. Help me make a plan that is actually fun and obviously broke without the broke being the theme.

### Assistant
The theme is Saturday with people you like. Broke is the constraint, not the invitation wording.

Plan that fits the money you named: pick a time window, pick a place that is already yours, pick food you can actually buy, pick one activity that does not require a ticket.

Time: afternoon into early evening, like 3 to 7. That dodges "dinner out" pressure and still feels like a birthday, not a homework hang.

Place: your place if that is allowed, or a park if weather is decent and your parent is okay with it. A living room with a speaker is not sad. Sad is an empty room with you refreshing a group chat about a photo booth you cannot enter. If home is messy, do a twenty-minute reset Friday night — dishes, bathroom, a clear table. That is set dressing, not a renovation.

Food: one cheap pizza, plus something from the pantry so it looks like a spread instead of a single box. If you have chips, fruit, leftover cake mix, popcorn, that is a table. Water and tap ice in a pitcher beats buying drinks for four. I will not invent prices. You already know pizza is in budget and the photo booth is not. Do not add a bakery cake if that blows the pizza. A boxed mix or store cookies are fine. Call it dessert, not "sorry cake."

Activity: movie you already have access to, a card game, a walk to a nearby place that is free, or a stupid tournament — who can build the tallest snack tower, playlist draft, drawing each other badly. One activity is enough. Three activities in a row is how a cheap party starts performing.

Invite text that does not apologize: "Birthday hang Saturday 3-7 at my place. Pizza. Bring yourself. If you want to bring a snack, cool, not required. No gifts." If you want gifts anyway, skip that last line. If you truly cannot do gifts, "no gifts" is a boundary, not a tragedy. Friends who care will show up with themselves.

### User
I'm worried two people will feel like a small number. And if I say no gifts they'll think I don't want them there. Also I don't have a speaker that works. The Bluetooth one died.

### Assistant
Two people is a hang. Three is a hang with a spare. A photo booth with twelve people can still feel lonely if nobody is talking to you. Small is not the same as sad. You can say "small on purpose" in your own head. You do not have to print it on a banner.

"No gifts" does not mean "no you." If you are scared of that reading, write: "Please don't bring presents. I just want you here." That is specific. People who almost get it will get it.

Dead speaker: phone speaker in a mug or a bowl is ugly and it works. A laptop on a chair works. Ask your parent if the TV can play a playlist or a movie. Borrow is allowed. Do not spend the pizza money on a new speaker the week of. The music is background. The people are the event.

If the third friend is a maybe, give them a clear yes-by time: "Need a yes by Friday noon so I order pizza." Maybes that last until Saturday afternoon are how you either over-order or sit waiting. You are allowed to plan for two and welcome a third.

Saturday run of show, still cheap: people arrive, pizza when it is hot, walk around the block or sit on the steps if you need air, movie or cards, cake-or-cookies, parent drives folks home. Take one picture if you want it, not a content shoot. If someone offers to pay you back for pizza, you can say yes for their share or no if that makes it weird. Your call. Do not fake a restaurant you cannot enter.

You are not required to perform gratitude for being poor. You are required to pick a time, a food, and two friends. That is a birthday. The photo booth can stay in the chat. You will be busy actually being there.
